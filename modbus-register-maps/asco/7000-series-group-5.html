ASCO 7000 Series Group 5 transfer switch controller (Modbus RTU) Modbus register map

Updated 17 November 2026

Short answer

The Group 5 controller runs ASCO 7000 Series automatic transfer switches. Through its Modbus RTU interface it reports normal and emergency source voltages and frequencies, switch position, source availability, phase rotation, alarms, the transfer-sequence state with its timer value, active features and the event log.

This page lists 35 Modbus registers for the ASCO 7000 Series Group 5 transfer switch controller (Modbus RTU) — 1 writable — with the table, 0-based wire address, data type, word order, scale, units and access of each, re-typed from ASCO’s own free documentation (linked below) and checked against it.

Integration notes: ASCO 7000 Series Group 5 transfer switch controller (Modbus RTU)

Integration in brief

Read 40015 (switch position and source availability), 40017-40024 (normal and emergency voltages), 40013/40014 (frequencies), 40040 (alarms) and 40041/40042 (transfer state and timer) with FC03, max 12 registers per read.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

Address and baud rate are set on the Group 5 controller; this document does not describe how (it refers to the Group 5 Controller User's Guide 381333-126).

Byte order & addressing

  • Maximum 12 registers per read; larger reads return an exception.
  • RTU only, 8 data bits, no parity, 1 stop bit; replies within 50 ms.

Network setup

  • Maximum 64 bytes per packet.

Watch out for

  • Wire addresses 0-10 and 55-99 (40001-40011, 40056-40100) are undefined; avoid reads that span them.

Notes on this map

  • Host: copy hosted by Contemporary Controls. Schneider Electric lists a newer revision (381339-221Q, document ASC-TS-UM-7000-PWRMNMDBS) on se.com, but the download is behind a bot check and was not opened; differences from revision I are unknown.
  • Readable addresses (decimal, wire): 11-54, 100, 105-173; at most 12 registers per FC03 read. Writable with FC06: 44 and 101-173 (time delays and settings, not listed here because their table is not in this revision's map).
  • All values are 16-bit. Voltages are whole volts, frequencies Hz x 100, phase shift degrees x 10 (signed).
  • The Power Manager Xp section of the same document (meter registers, LO/HO kWh words) is a separate device and is not part of this map.

Check it against the live device. A register map tells you what the controller can expose; the unit in front of you may differ by firmware or options. Read a few registers and compare them with the unit’s own display; for a 32-bit value the float and byte-order decoder shows all four word orders at once, so a wrong word order or scale is obvious. Questions about the ASCO 7000 Series Group 5 transfer switch controller (Modbus RTU)

What Modbus registers does the ASCO 7000 Series Group 5 transfer switch controller (Modbus RTU) have?

This map lists 35 registers: 35 holding registers. 1 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the ASCO 7000 Series Group 5 transfer switch controller (Modbus RTU) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers Modicon 3xxxx/4xxxx (drop the prefix, subtract 1). Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. The map says the address sent is the map address minus 40001, and the supported-address list (11-54, 100) matches the map's defined block 40012-40055 and 40101. The read example sends start address 0x0010 and gets the three normal voltages, which the map lists from 40017. The example prose names registers one lower (40016), but its hex addresses follow the map rule.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the ASCO 7000 Series Group 5 transfer switch controller (Modbus RTU)?

Start with HR 14 Switch position and source availability (Switch position and source availability); HR 16 NV_AB - normal voltage A-B (Normal voltage A-B); HR 20 EV_AB - emergency voltage A-B (Emergency voltage A-B); HR 12 NFRQ_0 - normal frequency (Normal frequency); HR 39 Alarm and lockout status (Alarm / lockout / engine running); HR 40 CPState - control panel state (Transfer sequence state).

How do I enable Modbus control of the ASCO 7000 Series Group 5 transfer switch controller (Modbus RTU)?

Address and baud rate are set on the Group 5 controller; this document does not describe how (it refers to the Group 5 Controller User's Guide 381333-126).

Where is the official ASCO 7000 Series Group 5 transfer switch controller (Modbus RTU) Modbus documentation?

The source is Connectivity to the ASCO Power Manager Xp & 7000 Series Group 5 Controller via Modbus (381339-221 I). ASCO doesn't publish it on its own website, so this map was re-typed from a free copy at https://ccontrols.com/support/dp/Asco%207000%20Group%205.pdf.
