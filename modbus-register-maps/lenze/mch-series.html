Short answer

The MCH is a Lenze AC Tech drive family that acts as a Modbus RTU slave at a fixed 9600 baud. Registers #1-#50 hold the control word, speed commands, status bytes, fault code, kWh and run-time counters and PID values; programming parameter n sits at register n + 51.

This page lists 24 Modbus registers for the Lenze MCH Series HVAC drive (Modbus RTU) — 10 writable — with the table, 0-based wire address, data type, word order, scale, units and access of each, re-typed from Lenze’s own free documentation (linked below) and checked against it.

Integration notes: Lenze MCH Series HVAC drive (Modbus RTU)

Integration in brief

Set P57 (serial link) and P58 (address) on the keypad. Read #26-#29 for load, status, mode, source and fault. To control: write 0 to #48 to unlock #1, or the password (default 19) to also unlock #40/#42; start/stop via #1 and write speed to #40, or set serial select (0200h on #1) and write #42.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

P57 SERIAL LINK = 01 (with 10 s watchdog) or 02 (without); P58 = address; P30 CONTROL sets how much the serial link may command (serial speed/auto modes). TB1 must be closed to unlock serial control.

Communication loss & watchdog

Fixed 10 s once #48 or #49 has unlocked the drive and P57 = with timer. Action depends on P30: normal modes drop out of serial control, serial speed modes follow P56 SERIAL LOSS, serial auto modes trip FLWR/SER fault.

Byte order & addressing

  • Read one register at a time except the #24 six-word block (and 4-word #100/#101).
  • FC16 accepts a single register only; FC03 and FC04 are identical.
  • No broadcast support.

Network setup

  • Fixed 9600 baud, 8 data bits, no parity, 2 stop bits.

Watch out for

  • If register #50 is not 148, do not write registers with this map.
  • End a session by writing 2 to #1 (lock security).

Notes on this map

  • Host: copy hosted by Precision Electric (a Lenze AC Tech distributor); no copy was found on lenze.com.
  • Left out: command speed #24 and actual speed #25. Table 6 prints 0.1 Hz (0-2400), while the data-type table and notes 3a/3b say hundredths of a Hz (0x0201 = 5.13 Hz); the guide contradicts itself. #24 can still be read as a 6-register block starting at address 24.
  • kWh: #32 is the low word and #33 the high word; total = high x 65536 + low. The guide reads one register at a time (only #24 and #100/#101 allow multi-word reads), so they are listed as two separate words.
  • Operational status (low byte #26): 0 fault lockout, 1 fault, 2 start pending, 3 stop, 4 DC brake, 5 run at 0 Hz, 6 run, 7 accel, 8 decel, 9 current limit, 10 decel override.
  • Speed source (high byte #28): 0 keypad, 1 0-10 VDC, 2 4-20 mA, 3-6 preset 1-4, 7 MOP, 8 keypad speed, 9 keypad/serial PID setpoint, 10 0-10 VDC PID setpoint, 11 4-20 mA PID setpoint, 12 serial speed, 13 serial PID setpoint, 14 sleep.
  • Present fault (high byte #29): 0 none, 1 output transistor, 3 high DC bus, 4 high drive temperature, 5 thermal overload, 7 low DC bus, 9 DC brake error, 10 serial/follower loss, 11 dynamic brake overload, 12 power sag, 13 control, 14 language, 15 external, 16 internal 16, 17 power transient, 18-23 internal errors.
  • Only the control registers and the three serial-setup parameters are listed; the full parameter list (#51 onward, one or two implied decimals) applies to software 213-070 rev 00 only.

Questions about the Lenze MCH Series HVAC drive (Modbus RTU)

What Modbus registers does the Lenze MCH Series HVAC drive (Modbus RTU) have?

This map lists 24 registers: 24 holding registers. 10 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Lenze MCH Series HVAC drive (Modbus RTU) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers derived from parameter numbers. Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. Lenze numbers by AC Tech register #, which is exactly the address sent on the wire (Modbus 4X/3X register numbers are one larger). The guide's frames confirm it: #24 is read with start address 0x0018. Programming parameter n = AC Tech register n + 51.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Lenze MCH Series HVAC drive (Modbus RTU)?

Start with HR 48 Unlock commands (Unlock control); HR 1 Drive control (Start / stop / serial select); HR 40 Keypad speed command (Keypad speed command); HR 27 Actual direction (high) / control mode (low) (Control mode (low byte) and direction); HR 26 Load (high byte) / operational status (low byte) (Load and status); HR 29 Present fault (high) / commanded direction (low) (Present fault).

How do I enable Modbus control of the Lenze MCH Series HVAC drive (Modbus RTU)?

P57 SERIAL LINK = 01 (with 10 s watchdog) or 02 (without); P58 = address; P30 CONTROL sets how much the serial link may command (serial speed/auto modes). TB1 must be closed to unlock serial control.

What happens when the Lenze MCH Series HVAC drive (Modbus RTU) loses Modbus communication?

Fixed 10 s once #48 or #49 has unlocked the drive and P57 = with timer. Action depends on P30: normal modes drop out of serial control, serial speed modes follow P56 SERIAL LOSS, serial auto modes trip FLWR/SER fault.

Where is the official Lenze MCH Series HVAC drive (Modbus RTU) Modbus documentation?
