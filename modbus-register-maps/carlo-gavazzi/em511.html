Short answer

The EM511 is a one-module single-phase energy meter (up to 45 A direct) with an RS485 Modbus RTU port and a digital input. Readings are 32-bit integers sent low word first with fixed weights. This map covers V, A, W, VA, var, PF, frequency, demand, THD, import/export and tariff energy, run hours, I/O and alarm status, alarm and tariff setup, serial settings and resets.

This page lists 64 Modbus registers for the Carlo Gavazzi EM511 single-phase energy meter (Modbus RTU) — 23 writable — with the table, 0-based wire address, data type, word order, scale, units and access of each, re-typed from Carlo Gavazzi’s own free documentation (linked below) and checked against it.

Integration notes: Carlo Gavazzi EM511 single-phase energy meter (Modbus RTU)

Integration in brief

Read input registers 0000h-0043h in one request (function 04 or 03). Decode INT32 low word first; V/W/VA/var x10, A x1000, energy x10, hours x100; PF and Hz are INT16 x1000 and x10.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

Modbus RTU on RS485 is always active; serial settings are at 2000h-2004h.

Byte order & addressing

  • 32-bit values are CDAB.
  • Stop-bit register uses 0 = 1 stop bit, 1 = 2 stop bits (unlike other Gavazzi meters).
  • Two PF conventions are published: 000Eh (IEC sign) and 0070h (L/C sign).

Network setup

  • Defaults: address 1, 9600 baud, no parity, 1 stop bit.
  • Max answer time 500 ms.

Watch out for

  • Total reset (4003h) is not available on MID versions.
  • Tariff selection by Modbus needs 1101h on and the digital input not set to tariff mode.

Notes on this map

  • Word order: INT32, UINT32 and UINT64 values are sent least significant word first, bytes MSB first (CDAB).
  • The document is labelled 'internal version' but is published on Carlo Gavazzi's own site.
  • Rows marked n.a. in the source always read 0 and are left out.
  • Left out: the mirror block at 0100h-017Eh, the INT64 energy block at 0500h-0532h (no word order stated for INT64), the run-hour/frequency rows that sit inside that block (0534h-053Eh), energy offsets (4100h-4210h), page filter and display settings, serial-number and name ASCII words, and reserved rows.
  • The part-number table shows hex 0702 for both PFB (1794) and SFA (1795); the decimal codes are used here.
  • 4020h is factory reset: write 0x0A0A then 0xC1A0 within one second.
  • Start-up current of run hour counter is printed as Modicon 304360 next to hex 110Bh; the hex address (wire 4363) is used.

Check it against the live device. A register map tells you what the controller can expose; the unit in front of you may differ by firmware or options. Read a few registers and compare them with the unit’s own display; for a 32-bit value the float and byte-order decoder shows all four word orders at once, so a wrong word order or scale is obvious. Questions about the Carlo Gavazzi EM511 single-phase energy meter (Modbus RTU)

What Modbus registers does the Carlo Gavazzi EM511 single-phase energy meter (Modbus RTU) have?

This map lists 64 registers: 39 input registers, 25 holding registers. 23 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Carlo Gavazzi EM511 single-phase energy meter (Modbus RTU) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers Modicon 3xxxx/4xxxx (drop the prefix, subtract 1). Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. Rows give a 6-digit Modicon number (3xxxxx, function 04) and the hex physical address used in the frame; 300001 / 0000h is wire address 0. Function 03 reads the same values (replace the leading 3 with 4).

What byte order does the Carlo Gavazzi EM511 single-phase energy meter (Modbus RTU) use for 32-bit values?

Multi-register values in this map use CDAB (low word first (word-swapped)). Set your client to this order for every 32/64-bit value in the table.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Carlo Gavazzi EM511 single-phase energy meter (Modbus RTU)?

Start with IR 0 V L-N (Voltage); IR 2 A (Current); IR 4 W (Active power); IR 14 PF (IEC convention) (Power factor); IR 15 Frequency (Frequency); IR 16 kWh (+) TOT (Imported kWh total); IR 32 kWh (-) TOT (Exported kWh total).

How do I enable Modbus control of the Carlo Gavazzi EM511 single-phase energy meter (Modbus RTU)?

Modbus RTU on RS485 is always active; serial settings are at 2000h-2004h.

Where is the official Carlo Gavazzi EM511 single-phase energy meter (Modbus RTU) Modbus documentation?

The source is EM511 Communication Protocol (rev. 1.8), revision 2023-05-23, published free by Carlo Gavazzi at https://www.gavazziautomation.com/fileadmin/images/PIM/OTHERSTUFF/COMPRO/EM511%20CP%20Rev1.8.pdf.
