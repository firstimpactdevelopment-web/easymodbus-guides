Short answer

Berner's IntelliSwitch Gen 5 air-curtain control exposes a small Modbus map over RS-485 (RTU or ASCII) and Ethernet/Wi-Fi (TCP). Input registers report door temperature, door switch, heater and motor state, E-stop lock and error codes; holding registers set mode (including a BMS mode), fan speed, target temperature, delays and BMS calls for heat and fan.

Integration notes: Berner Air curtain with IntelliSwitch Gen 5 controller (AIR BMS package)

Integration in brief

Put the controller in BMS mode (holding 101 = 5), then drive it with call for heat (107) and call for fan (108). Monitor door temperature (input 1), door switch (3), heater and motor state (6, 7) and E-stop (11).

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

The document points to the IntelliSwitch Gen 5 Quick Start Guide or the Berner app for Ethernet setup; it does not say where the serial settings are made.

Communication loss & watchdog

The serial settings include a timeout of 1-999 s; the document does not say what it does.

Byte order & addressing

  • Input and holding registers do not share addresses, by design.
  • Temperatures exist in both °C and °F registers.

Network setup

  • RS-485 for RTU/ASCII; Ethernet or Wi-Fi for TCP (DHCP by default).

Watch out for

  • While E-stop locked (input 11) is 1, mode and the BMS call registers are forced to 0 and read-only.

Notes on this map

  • All registers are signed 16-bit (INT16); temperatures are x10 (215 = 21.5 °C).
  • Serial settings are configurable (300-115200 bps, 5-8 data bits, 1-2 stop bits, none/odd/even/space/mark parity, ID 1-247, timeout 1-999 s with no stated purpose); the document does not state factory defaults. TCP defaults to DHCP and port 502.
  • Error code registers 201-206 hold codes without a code list in this document.

Check it against the live device. A register map tells you what the controller can expose; the unit in front of you may differ by firmware or options. Read a few registers and compare them with the unit’s own display; for a 32-bit value the float and byte-order decoder shows all four word orders at once, so a wrong word order or scale is obvious. Questions about the Berner Air curtain with IntelliSwitch Gen 5 controller (AIR BMS package)

What Modbus registers does the Berner Air curtain with IntelliSwitch Gen 5 controller (AIR BMS package) have?

This map lists 26 registers: 17 input registers, 9 holding registers. 9 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Berner Air curtain with IntelliSwitch Gen 5 controller (AIR BMS package) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers 1-based (subtract 1 for the wire address). Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. Berner lists addresses from 1 and says to send the offset from address 1: address 101 is requested as 100. Input and holding registers use separate address ranges (1-11/201-206 and 101-109).

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Berner Air curtain with IntelliSwitch Gen 5 controller (AIR BMS package)?

Start with HR 100 Mode (Mode (5 = BMS)); HR 106 Call for heat (BMS mode) (Call for heat in BMS mode); HR 107 Call for fan (BMS mode) (Call for fan in BMS mode); HR 102 Target temperature (°C) (Target temperature °C); IR 0 Temperature at the door (°C) (Door temperature °C); IR 2 Door switch status (Door switch); IR 10 E-stop locked (E-stop locked).
