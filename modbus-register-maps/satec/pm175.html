Short answer

The PM175 is a SATEC panel power meter and EN 50160 power quality analyzer with serial (RTU/ASCII) and optional Ethernet Modbus ports. Its 32-bit register set carries 1-second phase and total values, demands and energy counters as integers with fixed units, low word first. This map covers volts, amps, power, PF, THD, frequency, unbalance, present demands, energy totals, digital I/O, basic wiring/CT/PT setup, COM port settings, password and clear commands.

This page lists 109 Modbus registers for the SATEC PM175 powermeter and power quality analyzer — 17 writable — with the table, 0-based wire address, data type, word order, scale, units and access of each, re-typed from SATEC’s own free documentation (linked below) and checked against it.

Integration notes: SATEC PM175 powermeter and power quality analyzer

Integration in brief

Read 13952-14017 (phase values) and 14336-14361 (totals) with function 03; decode 32-bit integers low word first. Energy totals are at 14720 onward in whole kWh/kvarh.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

Choose the port protocol (Modbus RTU/ASCII) and address in the COM setup (2344-2359) or from the front panel; Modbus/TCP runs on the Ethernet option.

Byte order & addressing

  • 32-bit values are low word first (CDAB).
  • Voltage and power units change from 0.1 V / 1 W to 1 V / 1 kW when a PT ratio above 1 is set.
  • 120 user-assignable registers (0-119, mapped by 120-239) let you pack scattered values into one read.

Network setup

  • Set wiring mode, PT ratio and CT primary at 2304-2306 first (and the PT ratio factor at 2324 if used).
  • Write the password to 2575 if protection is enabled.

Watch out for

  • Changing the PT ratio from 1 changes the meaning of every voltage and power register.
  • Broadcast (address 0) is not supported.

Notes on this map

  • Addressing: the guide says to add 40001 to a register address to get the Modicon holding-register number (400001 above 9999), so printed addresses are 0-based wire addresses.
  • Word order: 32-bit values are sent with the low-order word in the first (even) register and the high-order word in the second, so they are CDAB here.
  • Units U1 (voltage) and U3 (power) depend on the PT ratio: with PT ratio = 1 they are 0.1 V and 0.001 kW/kvar/kVA (the scales used here); with PT ratio > 1 they become 1 V and 1 kW/kvar/kVA. U2 (current) is always 0.01 A.
  • Function 04 can be used the same way as function 03.
  • Voltage rows marked V1/V12 read line-to-neutral in 4LN3, 3LN3 and 3BLN3 wiring and line-to-line otherwise.
  • Energy counters are whole kWh/kvarh/kVAh and roll over at the energy roll value set in the device options.
  • Left out: 16-bit scaled registers (need per-site HI/LO scale arithmetic), 1-cycle and 1/2-cycle values, harmonics, min/max logs, TOU registers, the total PF lag/lead rows (printed as UINT16 in a two-register slot), setpoint, log and file-transfer registers.
  • When password protection is on, write the password to 2575 before any setup or clear command, otherwise writes fail with exception 01.

Check it against the live device. A register map tells you what the controller can expose; the unit in front of you may differ by firmware or options. Read a few registers and compare them with the unit’s own display; for a 32-bit value the float and byte-order decoder shows all four word orders at once, so a wrong word order or scale is obvious. Questions about the SATEC PM175 powermeter and power quality analyzer

What Modbus registers does the SATEC PM175 powermeter and power quality analyzer have?

This map lists 109 registers: 109 holding registers. 17 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the SATEC PM175 powermeter and power quality analyzer Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers 0-based (the number printed is the address on the wire). Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. The guide numbers registers 0-65535 and says to add 40001 (or 400001 above 9999) for Modicon holding-register numbers, so the printed address goes straight into the frame: 13952 is wire address 13952 (Modicon 413953).

What byte order does the SATEC PM175 powermeter and power quality analyzer use for 32-bit values?

Multi-register values in this map use CDAB (low word first (word-swapped)). Set your client to this order for every 32/64-bit value in the table.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the SATEC PM175 powermeter and power quality analyzer?

Start with HR 13952 V1/V12 Voltage (V1/V12 voltage); HR 13958 I1 Current (I1 current); HR 14336 Total kW (Total kW); HR 14342 Total PF (Total PF); HR 14468 Frequency (Frequency); HR 14720 kWh import (kWh import); HR 14722 kWh export (kWh export).

How do I enable Modbus control of the SATEC PM175 powermeter and power quality analyzer?

Choose the port protocol (Modbus RTU/ASCII) and address in the COM setup (2344-2359) or from the front panel; Modbus/TCP runs on the Ethernet option.
