Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Summary

easymodbus.com is a static website: register maps, guides and browser tools. There are no accounts, no sign-up and no forms, and nothing you type into the tools leaves your browser. The site uses Google Analytics, but only after you accept the cookie banner — see Website analytics.

Website analytics

easymodbus.com uses Google Analytics (GA4) to understand which pages are useful and how visitors arrive. It is loaded with Google Consent Mode: no analytics cookie is set and no data is sent until you choose Accept on the cookie banner. If you decline, or ignore the banner, Analytics stays off. Your choice is remembered in your browser and you can clear it any time by clearing this site's data. IP addresses are anonymised, and no advertising or cross-site tracking signals are collected. Google's handling of Analytics data is described in Google's Privacy Policy.

Hosting and fonts

The site is served by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes the technical details of each request — your IP address, the page requested, your browser's user-agent — to deliver the page and protect the site from abuse; see Cloudflare's privacy policy. Page fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when it serves them.

The browser tools

The float decoder, the address converter and the register filters on each map run entirely in your browser. The values you type are not sent anywhere.

Downloads

The CSV and JSON register-map downloads are plain static files. Downloading one does not require or record anything beyond the ordinary request described under Hosting.

Email

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply. We do not add you to any list.

What the site does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or registration.
  • No advertising or cross-site tracking.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.

Children

This site is a reference for building and industrial equipment and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the “last updated” date above changes with it.
