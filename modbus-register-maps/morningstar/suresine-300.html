Short answer

The SureSine-300 is a Morningstar sine-wave inverter (115 V and 220 V versions). Through its RJ-11 meter port and Morningstar's MSC adapter it speaks Modbus RTU: battery voltage, heatsink temperature, fault/alarm bits, load state and nominal output settings in RAM, a remote-off and reset register, and EEPROM custom disconnect/reconnect voltages.

Integration notes: Morningstar SureSine-300 off-grid sine-wave inverter (Modbus RTU via MeterBus)

Integration in brief

Via the MSC adapter at 9600 8N2, ID 1, read 0x0000-0x000E: battery voltage (x 16.92/65536), heatsink temperature, faults, alarms and load state. Write a non-zero value to 0x000F to switch the AC output off remotely.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

No enable step; connect a Morningstar MSC MeterBus-to-RS-232 adapter to the meter port.

Byte order & addressing

  • The disconnect state (load state 5) is only entered through the meter or the m_disconnect register.
  • Output voltage and frequency registers show the nominal setting, not live measurements.

Network setup

  • Serial: 9600 baud, 8 data bits, no parity, 2 stop bits, no flow control.

Watch out for

  • Reset the inverter after EEPROM writes to clear the settings-edit fault.

Notes on this map

  • Voltage scaling is n x 16.92 x 2^-16 (V03 corrected this from 2^-15), converted to the multiplier 16.92/65536.
  • Left out: AC output current 0x0001/0x0005, because its scale depends on the version (full scale 6.4 A on 220 V units, 17 A on 115/127 V units: n x 6.4 or 17 x 2^-15). Also the raw thermistor and remote-on voltages (no scaling given), the hourmeter 0xE040 and serial number 0xE044-0xE047 (size and word order not stated).
  • The parameters page still says 'TriStar' (copied text); the defaults it gives (9600 8N2, ID 1) are for the SureSine.
  • The definitions section repeats several addresses (e.g. hertz and volts both 0x000D, EEPROM entries shifted by one, with LVD warning timer 2 described as a voltage); the register tables are followed here.
  • Any EEPROM write sets an 'EEPROM changed' fault until the inverter is reset; writes are not verified. FC 0x03/0x04 read, FC 0x06 writes.

Check it against the live device. A register map tells you what the controller can expose; the unit in front of you may differ by firmware or options. Read a few registers and compare them with the unit’s own display; for a 32-bit value the float and byte-order decoder shows all four word orders at once, so a wrong word order or scale is obvious. Questions about the Morningstar SureSine-300 off-grid sine-wave inverter (Modbus RTU via MeterBus)

What Modbus registers does the Morningstar SureSine-300 off-grid sine-wave inverter (Modbus RTU via MeterBus) have?

This map lists 24 registers: 24 holding registers. 12 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Morningstar SureSine-300 off-grid sine-wave inverter (Modbus RTU via MeterBus) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers hexadecimal, 0-based. Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. The spec says all listed addresses are request-PDU addresses, so the hex value in the PDU column goes on the wire unchanged (logical address = PDU + 1).

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Morningstar SureSine-300 off-grid sine-wave inverter (Modbus RTU via MeterBus)?

Start with HR 4 Vb - battery voltage, filtered (Battery voltage); HR 11 Load state (Load state); HR 7 Fault bitfield (Faults); HR 8 Alarm bitfield (Alarms); HR 6 Ths - heatsink temperature (Heatsink temperature); HR 15 m_disconnect - remote off (Remote AC off).

How do I enable Modbus control of the Morningstar SureSine-300 off-grid sine-wave inverter (Modbus RTU via MeterBus)?

No enable step; connect a Morningstar MSC MeterBus-to-RS-232 adapter to the meter port.
