Short answer

The XR10CX is a digital operating control used on PVI water heaters. It is a Modbus RTU slave on 2-wire RS-485 at a fixed 9600 8N1, with a handful of holding registers for temperature and set points and output coils for on/off, keypad lock, alarms and the call-for-heat relay.

Integration notes: PVI Industries XR10CX water heater digital operating controller (Modbus RTU)

Integration in brief

Poll 9600 8N1. Read probe 1 at 40257 and the set point at 40864 with FC03; read status coils 513-545 with FC01, and switch the control on/off with coil 513.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

Modbus RTU is available through the XJ485 serial interface connected to the XR10CX programming port.

Byte order & addressing

  • Maximum 5 registers per request.
  • Register and coil numbers are Modicon-style; subtract 40001 for registers and 1 for coils.
  • Coils can be read up to 80 at a time but written only one at a time.

Network setup

  • Fixed serial settings: 9600 baud, 8 data bits, no parity, 1 stop bit (not adjustable).
  • Set the slave address (1-247) on the keypad; each unit on the bus needs its own address.
  • Leave at least 500 ms before a retry. The controller has no bias resistors; bias the line at the master.

Watch out for

  • In OFF the display shows OFF and outputs are open, but temperatures can still be read.

Notes on this map

  • PVI does not host this manual publicly that we could find; this copy is served by a controls distributor and is the unaltered PVI document (PVI Industries footer, document number on every page).
  • Temperatures are whole degrees F per section 4.2.
  • The manual is inconsistent about coil 513: Table 2 lists it as a Read/Write coil, while section 4.2 says ON/OFF is written as 257/1 and calls it write-only. Treat it as a standard on/off coil and confirm on site.
  • Units for the differential and alarm delays are not stated in the table.

Check it against the live device. A register map tells you what the controller can expose; the unit in front of you may differ by firmware or options. Read a few registers and compare them with the unit’s own display; for a 32-bit value the float and byte-order decoder shows all four word orders at once, so a wrong word order or scale is obvious. Questions about the PVI Industries XR10CX water heater digital operating controller (Modbus RTU)

What Modbus registers does the PVI Industries XR10CX water heater digital operating controller (Modbus RTU) have?

This map lists 22 registers: 15 coils, 7 holding registers. 9 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the PVI Industries XR10CX water heater digital operating controller (Modbus RTU) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers Modicon 3xxxx/4xxxx (drop the prefix, subtract 1). Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. The manual uses register numbers: holding register 40001 is address 0 on the wire, so subtract 40001 (40769 -> 768). Coils are numbered from 00001 the same way, so coil 513 is wire address 512.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the PVI Industries XR10CX water heater digital operating controller (Modbus RTU)?

Start with HR 256 Probe 1 temperature (PRB1) (Probe 1 temperature); HR 863 System set point (Set) (System set point); CO 542 Relay call for heat (Call for heat relay); CO 537 Any-alarm (can be muted) (Any-alarm); CO 512 On/off for control (On/off for control); CO 520 Probe 1 error (Probe 1 error).

How do I enable Modbus control of the PVI Industries XR10CX water heater digital operating controller (Modbus RTU)?

Modbus RTU is available through the XJ485 serial interface connected to the XR10CX programming port.

Where is the official PVI Industries XR10CX water heater digital operating controller (Modbus RTU) Modbus documentation?

The source is MODBUS-RTU Applied to the XR10CX Control (PV500-67 06/2014), revision 2014-06. PVI Industries doesn't publish it on its own website, so this map was re-typed from a free copy at https://www.ccontrols.com/support/dp/PV500-67.pdf. It may be an older revision, so check it against the live unit.
