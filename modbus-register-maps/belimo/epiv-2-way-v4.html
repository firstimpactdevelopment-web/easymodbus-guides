Belimo 2-way EPIV DN 15-50 (Version 4.2) Modbus RTU Modbus register map

Updated 28 October 2026

Short answer

Belimo's electronic pressure-independent valve measures flow with its own sensor and controls either position or flow. On Modbus RTU it takes a relative setpoint and override, and reports position, flow in l/s, gpm and a selectable unit, medium temperature, glycol and accumulated volume, plus fault flags and the min/max flow limits. 32-bit values are low word first.

This page lists 47 Modbus registers for the Belimo 2-way EPIV DN 15-50 (Version 4.2) Modbus RTU — 18 writable — with the table, 0-based wire address, data type, word order, scale, units and access of each, re-typed from Belimo’s own free documentation (linked below) and checked against it.

Integration notes: Belimo 2-way EPIV DN 15-50 (Version 4.2) Modbus RTU

Integration in brief

Set address and frame with Belimo Assistant 2, write Setpoint source No. 119 = 1 (Bus), choose Control mode No. 117, then write the setpoint to No. 1 (address 0) and poll flow (No. 8 or 10), position (No. 5) and faults (No. 105).

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

Configure Modbus address (1-247), baud and frame with Belimo Assistant 2, then write No. 119 = 1 so the valve follows the bus setpoint.

Communication loss & watchdog

No. 110 sets the timeout (5-3600 s, default 120 s). If neither No. 1 nor No. 2 is written in that time, the valve performs the Bus fail action in No. 109 (default None) and bit 15 of No. 105 is set.

Byte order & addressing

  • Register No. is 1-based; subtract 1 for the wire address.
  • Changing Control mode (No. 117) resets the device.
  • 32-bit pairs are low word first and must be written with one FC 16 covering only that pair.

Network setup

  • Default frame 1-8-N-2 at 38400 baud, address 1.
  • Up to 32 nodes per segment without a repeater; terminate with 120 ohm.

Watch out for

  • Out of the box the valve listens to the analog input, not the bus.
  • 65535 means 'not available'.

Notes on this map

  • 32-bit values occupy two registers with the low word first (Belimo's worked example: No. 10 = 14551, No. 11 = 19 gives 19 x 65536 + 14551 = 1,259,735), so they are UINT32 with CDAB word order. Write a 32-bit pair with one FC 16 request covering only those two registers.
  • Values are unsigned unless Belimo marks them signed (**): the flow-body temperatures (No. 22/23) are signed two's complement and typed INT16.
  • The flow-meter serial-number pairs (No. 201/202, 203/204) are marked LowWord/HighWord in the register overview, so they are typed UINT32 low word first.
  • Unsupported registers read 65535. Writable registers from No. 101 up are persistent; don't write them cyclically.
  • Belimo notes that the HVAC application may clamp values inside the listed ranges depending on the valve size (see the product datasheet).
  • Unlike the air/water actuators, Setpoint source (No. 119) defaults to Analog on this valve: write 1 before bus setpoints take effect.

Check it against the live device. A register map tells you what the controller can expose; the unit in front of you may differ by firmware or options. Questions about the Belimo 2-way EPIV DN 15-50 (Version 4.2) Modbus RTU

What Modbus registers does the Belimo 2-way EPIV DN 15-50 (Version 4.2) Modbus RTU have?

This map lists 47 registers: 47 holding registers. 18 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Belimo 2-way EPIV DN 15-50 (Version 4.2) Modbus RTU Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers 1-based (subtract 1 for the wire address). Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. Belimo prints each register twice: 'No.' counts from 1 and 'Address' from 0, and the documented value here is the No. column. Register No. 1 (setpoint) is wire address 0. Every value sits in one flat table, so holding-register reads (FC 3) and writes (FC 6/16) reach everything; FC 4 input-register reads of the same addresses also work.

What byte order does the Belimo 2-way EPIV DN 15-50 (Version 4.2) Modbus RTU use for 32-bit values?

Multi-register values in this map use CDAB (low word first (word-swapped)). Set your client to this order for every 32/64-bit value in the table.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Belimo 2-way EPIV DN 15-50 (Version 4.2) Modbus RTU?

Start with HR 118 Setpoint source (Setpoint source = Bus (default is Analog)); HR 116 Control mode (Position or flow control); HR 0 Setpoint relative (Setpoint, 0.01 %); HR 7 Absolute volumetric flow (l/s) (Flow l/s); HR 4 Relative position (Relative position); HR 21 Temperature flow body (°C) (Medium temperature °C); HR 104 Malfunction and service information (Fault flags); HR 109 Timeout for communication watchdog (Watchdog timeout).

How do I enable Modbus control of the Belimo 2-way EPIV DN 15-50 (Version 4.2) Modbus RTU?

Configure Modbus address (1-247), baud and frame with Belimo Assistant 2, then write No. 119 = 1 so the valve follows the bus setpoint.

What happens when the Belimo 2-way EPIV DN 15-50 (Version 4.2) Modbus RTU loses Modbus communication?

No. 110 sets the timeout (5-3600 s, default 120 s). If neither No. 1 nor No. 2 is written in that time, the valve performs the Bus fail action in No. 109 (default None) and bit 15 of No. 105 is set.
