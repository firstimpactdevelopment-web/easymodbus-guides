Easy Modbus · privacy policy

Privacy policy

Effective 30 September 2026 · Last updated: 30 September 2026

Summary

The Easy Modbus app has no account and no analytics, and the developer runs no server: everything you build in the app stays on your phone and goes only to the equipment on the network you connect it to. Two Google services are involved, and only these two: Google AdMob, which shows the free version's optional rewarded video ads (and may use your device's advertising ID), and Google Play Billing, which handles the one-time purchase that removes ads. This website (easymodbus.com) uses Google Analytics, but only after you accept the cookie banner — see Website analytics.

What the app stores, and where

Easy Modbus saves the following on your phone only, in the app's private storage:

  • The devices you add — host name or IP address, port, unit (slave) ID, framing (Modbus TCP or RTU over TCP), timeouts, and the name you give each one.
  • The register maps you build — addresses, data types, word order, scaling, units, named states, write limits and notes.
  • Your custom remotes and control panels: the controls you place and the registers they read and write. A control panel also keeps the last-known address (host, port, unit ID) of every device it drives, so it can still reach them if a device is later removed from your list.
  • Your settings: Easy or Pro mode, language, the last port you used, and whether you have accepted the Terms of Use.
  • Whether this install has the one-time unlock (cached so it keeps working offline).

The app sets allowBackup="false" and excludes all of this from Android cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, because a register map or panel names a customer's equipment and the addresses that command it. That means it does not follow you to a new phone; the exports below are the way to move it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version offers short rewarded video ads, supplied by Google AdMob, before exporting a report and before opening a saved custom remote. The app always asks first — Watch ad, Remove ads or Cancel — and never starts an ad on its own. If no ad can be loaded (no signal, no consent, nothing to show) the action simply goes ahead.

To serve ads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK may collect your device's advertising ID (the app declares the AD_ID permission for this), your IP address, and standard ad-delivery and diagnostic information. How Google uses that data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. The developer receives only aggregate earnings figures from AdMob — never your data. No ads are requested before you have accepted the Terms of Use, and none are shown after the one-time purchase.

Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where the law requires it, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent before any ad is requested; you may refuse personalised ads and still use everything. The consent form appears only after you accept the Terms of Use. You can change your answer at any time from Ad privacy choices in the app's menu (on the main screen and on the Control Panels screen), which appears wherever UMP says it is required.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The one-time in-app purchase (“Remove ads & unlimited remotes”) is processed entirely by Google Play. The app asks Play whether this account owns the purchase so a reinstall gets it back, and records the answer on the phone. The developer never sees your payment details. Google's handling of purchase data is covered by Google Play's own terms and privacy policy.

What leaves the phone

  • Modbus requests to the equipment you point the app at, on your local network (or over a VPN you set up). Finding devices sweeps your own subnet for port 502 — the app explains this before every scan.
  • Exports you ask for, handed to whichever app you choose (email, Drive, and so on): the Export Report CSV of every device and reading with live values; a single device's register map as CSV (Export this map); and a control panel as a .empanel file (a JSON layout including each device's address and unit ID), when you long-press a panel. Easy Modbus has no server and uploads none of these anywhere.
  • Google AdMob, UMP and Play Billing traffic, as described above.

Website analytics

This website, easymodbus.com, uses Google Analytics (GA4) to understand which guides are useful and how visitors arrive. It is loaded with Google Consent Mode: no analytics cookie is set and no data is sent until you choose Accept on the cookie banner. If you decline, or ignore the banner, Analytics stays off. Your choice is remembered in your browser and you can clear it any time by clearing this site's data. IP addresses are anonymised, and no advertising or cross-site tracking signals are collected on the website. Google's handling of Analytics data is described in Google's Privacy Policy. This is separate from the app, which contains no analytics at all.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, or registration.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting inside the app itself. (The website uses Google Analytics with consent — see Website analytics above.)
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or file-storage permission. Imports and exports go through Android's own file picker and share sheet.
  • No selling or sharing of your data by the developer.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state — to open TCP connections to Modbus equipment, to find out which local network the phone is on so it can be swept, and to keep that traffic on Wi-Fi or Ethernet when the network has no internet. Also used by the ad and billing services.
  • Wi-Fi state — to read the local address and subnet.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID, and the related Android ad-services permissions added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK) — used only by AdMob, as described above.
  • Google Play billing — for the one-time purchase.

Your choices

  • Decline any ad prompt with Cancel; nothing plays unless you choose Watch ad.
  • Change your ad-consent answer any time from Ad privacy choices in the app's menu (where that applies).
  • Reset or delete your advertising identifier, or turn off ad personalisation, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • The one-time purchase removes advertising entirely.
  • To erase everything the app has stored, uninstall it or clear its storage — nothing is kept anywhere else.

Children

Easy Modbus is a tool for building and industrial equipment and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
