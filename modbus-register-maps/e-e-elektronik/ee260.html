Short answer

The EE260 is an M12-connected humidity and temperature probe for weather stations, with two analogue outputs and an RS-485 Modbus RTU port. It publishes every measured and calculated humidity quantity twice: as 32-bit floats and as scaled 16-bit integers, in metric and US units. This map lists both blocks.

Integration notes: E+E Elektronik EE260 humidity and temperature probe for meteorology (RS-485 Modbus RTU)

Integration in brief

Connect at 9600 baud, 8E1, address 235, and read two registers from 0x3EA for temperature (float, low word first) and from 0x3FC for relative humidity. If the master cannot swap words, read the scaled integers at 0xFA1 (T x100) and 0xFAA (RH x100).

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

Address, baud rate, parity and stop bits are set with the free PCS10 software through the HA011018 adapter and HA011020 cable.

Byte order & addressing

  • Floats are low word first (CDAB).
  • Integer °F temperature uses divisor 50, not 100.
  • Register numbers in the guide are 1-based; subtract 1.

Network setup

  • Factory settings: 9600 baud, 8 data bits, even parity, 1 stop bit, address 235.
  • Selectable: 9600-115200 baud, none/odd/even parity, 1 or 2 stop bits, address 1-247.
  • Supply 7-30 V DC; RS-485 A (D+) is pin 6, B (D-) is pin 2 on the M12 plug.

Watch out for

  • The factory address is 235, not 1.
  • Use a shielded cable to stay inside the EMC figures.

Notes on this map

  • Float word order comes from E+E application note AN0103 Rev. 1.4 (11/2020), section 7.2, which the quick guide points to: the two byte pairs of a float are swapped (bytes 3-4 first, then 1-2), i.e. low word first (CDAB). The note's write example (1013.25 mbar sent as 49 9A 44 7D) also puts the high word 44 7D second. AN0103: https://downloads.epluse.com/fileadmin/data/product/ee071/AN0103.pdf
  • Integer scaling: the quick guide's scale column is a divisor (scale 100: a reading of 2550 = 25.5; scale 50: 2550 = 51). The scale field here is the matching multiplier, so the ppm row with divisor 0.1 becomes x10.
  • Measured values can be read with function 03 or 04 (both are listed); they are filed here as holding registers.
  • Left out: the slave-address register (0x00) and communication-settings register (0x01). AN0103 says they are write-only and does not fully define the settings byte coding.
  • Read each float with one request for both registers, so both halves come from the same measurement (AN0103 advice).

Check it against the live device. A register map tells you what the controller can expose; the unit in front of you may differ by firmware or options. Read a few registers and compare them with the unit’s own display; for a 32-bit value the float and byte-order decoder shows all four word orders at once, so a wrong word order or scale is obvious. Questions about the E+E Elektronik EE260 humidity and temperature probe for meteorology (RS-485 Modbus RTU)

What Modbus registers does the E+E Elektronik EE260 humidity and temperature probe for meteorology (RS-485 Modbus RTU) have?

This map lists 52 registers: 52 holding registers. 0 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the E+E Elektronik EE260 humidity and temperature probe for meteorology (RS-485 Modbus RTU) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers 1-based (subtract 1 for the wire address). Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. The quick guide prints a decimal register number that starts at 1 and a hex register address that starts at 0 (1003 = 0x3EA). Send the hex address on the wire: register number minus 1.

What byte order does the E+E Elektronik EE260 humidity and temperature probe for meteorology (RS-485 Modbus RTU) use for 32-bit values?

Multi-register values in this map use CDAB (low word first (word-swapped)). Set your client to this order for every 32/64-bit value in the table.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the E+E Elektronik EE260 humidity and temperature probe for meteorology (RS-485 Modbus RTU)?

Start with HR 1002 Temperature T (°C) (Temperature, float °C); HR 1020 Relative humidity RH (%RH) (Relative humidity, float %RH); HR 1104 Dew point temperature Td (°C) (Dew point, float °C); HR 4001 Temperature T (°C, integer) (Temperature, integer °C x100); HR 4010 Relative humidity RH (%RH, integer) (Relative humidity, integer x100).

How do I enable Modbus control of the E+E Elektronik EE260 humidity and temperature probe for meteorology (RS-485 Modbus RTU)?

Address, baud rate, parity and stop bits are set with the free PCS10 software through the HA011018 adapter and HA011020 cable.
