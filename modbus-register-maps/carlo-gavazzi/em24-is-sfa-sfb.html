Short answer

The SFA and SFB versions of the EM24-DIN are reduced-function meters whose RS485 Modbus RTU map exposes only voltages, currents, power factor, frequency, phase sequence and imported active energy, plus status and serial settings. Values are 32-bit integers sent low word first with fixed weights.

Integration notes: Carlo Gavazzi EM24-IS (SFA and SFB models)

Integration in brief

Read input registers (04 or 03) in blocks of up to 11; decode INT32 low word first. Energy is only kWh(+) total at 003Eh, x10.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

Modbus RTU is on the IS (RS485) versions. Set the address and baud rate in the serial port menu or via registers 110Ah/110Bh.

Byte order & addressing

  • INT32 values are low word first (CDAB).
  • Max 11 registers per read.
  • Parity fixed to none, 1 stop bit.

Network setup

  • Address 1-247, baud 4800 or 9600.
  • Terminate both bus ends with 120 ohm.

Watch out for

  • No power or reactive registers exist on these versions; they read as reserved.

Notes on this map

  • Word order: the document states INT32/UINT32/UINT64 are sent LSW first (bytes MSB first), so 32-bit values are CDAB.
  • Functions 03 and 04 are equivalent; at most 11 registers per request.
  • The identification code is printed at 000Bh, which is also the second word of V L3-L1; read it on its own.
  • All other rows in the measurement table are marked reserved in this document and are left out, as are the reserved configuration and reset rows and the ASCII serial-number letters.

Check it against the live device. A register map tells you what the controller can expose; the unit in front of you may differ by firmware or options. Questions about the Carlo Gavazzi EM24-IS (SFA and SFB models)

What Modbus registers does the Carlo Gavazzi EM24-IS (SFA and SFB models) have?

This map lists 30 registers: 22 input registers, 8 holding registers. 8 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Carlo Gavazzi EM24-IS (SFA and SFB models) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers Modicon 3xxxx/4xxxx (drop the prefix, subtract 1). Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. Each register is printed twice: a 6-digit Modicon number for function 04 (3xxxxx; the same data is readable with function 03 by changing the leading 3 to 4) and the hex 'physical address' that goes in the frame. Wire address = physical address, e.g. 300001 / 0000h is address 0. Configuration rows are also printed as 3xxxxx but are written with function 06, so they are listed here as holding registers.

What byte order does the Carlo Gavazzi EM24-IS (SFA and SFB models) use for 32-bit values?

Multi-register values in this map use CDAB (low word first (word-swapped)). Set your client to this order for every 32/64-bit value in the table.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Carlo Gavazzi EM24-IS (SFA and SFB models)?

Start with IR 0 V L1-N (V L1-N); IR 12 A L1 (A L1); IR 53 PF system (PF system); IR 55 Frequency (Frequency); IR 62 kWh(+) total (kWh(+) total).

How do I enable Modbus control of the Carlo Gavazzi EM24-IS (SFA and SFB models)?

Modbus RTU is on the IS (RS485) versions. Set the address and baud rate in the serial port menu or via registers 110Ah/110Bh.

Where is the official Carlo Gavazzi EM24-IS (SFA and SFB models) Modbus documentation?

The source is EM24-IS SFA and SFB versions Communication Protocol (Revision 2.0), published free by Carlo Gavazzi at https://www.gavazziautomation.com/fileadmin/images/PIM/OTHERSTUFF/COMPRO/EM24_IS_SFA_SFB.pdf.
