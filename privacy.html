Easy Modbus · privacy policy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 September 2026

Summary

Easy Modbus has no account and no analytics, and the app itself sends nothing to any server. Everything it does happens on your phone and on the local network you connect it to. The one exception is advertising: the free version shows occasional rewarded video ads from Google AdMob, described below, and the one-time purchase removes them.

What the app stores, and where

Easy Modbus saves the following on your phone, and nowhere else:

  • The devices you add — address, port, unit ID and name.
  • The register maps you build — addresses, data types, scaling, units and notes.
  • Any custom control screens you create.
  • Your choice of Easy or Pro mode.

This data is deliberately excluded from Android cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer, because a register map names a customer's equipment. That does mean it does not follow you to a new phone; the CSV export is the way to move a map.

Advertising

The free version shows occasional rewarded video ads, supplied by Google AdMob, in exchange for exporting a report or opening a saved custom remote. To serve them, Google's advertising SDK may collect a device advertising identifier and standard ad-delivery information. How Google uses that data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before any personalised ad, and may choose non-personalised ads instead. The one-time in-app purchase removes all advertising and also unlocks unlimited custom remotes. The developer receives only aggregate, anonymous earnings figures from AdMob — never your data.

What leaves the phone

Two things, both only when you ask for them:

  • Modbus requests to the equipment you point the app at, on your local network.
  • A CSV export, when you tap Export. The file is written to the app's cache and handed to whichever app you choose to send it with. Easy Modbus has no server and does not upload it anywhere.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in, or registration.
  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting. (The free version shows Google AdMob ads — see Advertising below.)
  • No location, contacts, photos, or microphone access.
  • No internet use beyond talking to the equipment you point it at.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state — to open TCP connections to Modbus equipment, and to find out which local network the phone is on so it can be swept.
  • Wi-Fi state — to read the local address and subnet.

Your choices

  • You can reset or delete your advertising identifier, or turn off ad personalisation, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Where a consent prompt applies, you can change your answer at any time; declining simply leaves you with non-personalised ads.
  • The one-time purchase removes advertising entirely.
  • To erase everything the app has stored, uninstall it — nothing is kept anywhere else.

Children

Easy Modbus is a tool for building and industrial equipment and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the “last updated” date above changes with it. Material changes will be reflected here before they take effect in a new release.
