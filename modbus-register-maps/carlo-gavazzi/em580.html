Short answer

The EM580 is a DIN-rail three-phase AC energy meter for AC charging stations, with signed (Eichrecht) energy data and an RS485 Modbus RTU port. Readings are 32-bit integers sent low word first with fixed weights. This map covers per-phase and system V, A, W, VA, var, PF, frequency, energy totals, the device state word, serial settings and the main OCMF charging-session registers.

Integration notes: Carlo Gavazzi EM580 three-phase AC energy meter for EV chargers (Modbus RTU)

Integration in brief

Read input registers 0000h-0057h in one request (function 04 or 03): INT32 values low word first, V/W/VA/var x10, A x1000, PF and Hz as INT16 x1000 and x10. Use 7040h/7045h/7100h for OCMF signed sessions.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

Modbus RTU on the RS485 port is always active; set address and line settings at 2000h-2004h and write 1 to 2010h to apply.

Byte order & addressing

  • 32-bit values are CDAB.
  • PF sign shows import/export; the separate L/C words give lead/lag.

Network setup

  • Defaults: address 1, 9600 baud, no parity, 1 stop bit.
  • Max answer time 500 ms, typical 40 ms.

Watch out for

  • Write 0 to 7100h after reading the OCMF file or a new session will not start.
  • Sync the clock (7031h/7032h) at least every 24 h for signed data.

Notes on this map

  • Word order: INT32, UINT32 and UINT64 values are least significant word first with bytes MSB first (CDAB).
  • Left out: the mirror INT32 block at 0102h-0146h (same values, different layout), INT64 energy at 0500h-052Ch and the INT64 UTC timestamp at 7032h (no stated word order for signed 64-bit), the 32-bit frequency at 053Ch (part of the INT64 block), signed OBIS maps at 0800h, public keys and signatures, serial number and device tag ASCII words, page filters, OCMF identification and custom-string fields, the OCMF buffer, maintenance mode and the cable-loss datalogger.
  • The display shows EEE when the high word of a variable is 7FFFh (over range).
  • No total-energy reset register is published for this meter; 4020h is factory reset (write 0x0A0A, then 0xC1A0 within 5 s).

Check it against the live device. A register map tells you what the controller can expose; the unit in front of you may differ by firmware or options. Read a few registers and compare them with the unit’s own display; for a 32-bit value the float and byte-order decoder shows all four word orders at once, so a wrong word order or scale is obvious. Questions about the Carlo Gavazzi EM580 three-phase AC energy meter for EV chargers (Modbus RTU)

What Modbus registers does the Carlo Gavazzi EM580 three-phase AC energy meter for EV chargers (Modbus RTU) have?

This map lists 68 registers: 42 input registers, 26 holding registers. 15 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Carlo Gavazzi EM580 three-phase AC energy meter for EV chargers (Modbus RTU) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers Modicon 3xxxx/4xxxx (drop the prefix, subtract 1). Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. Rows give a 6-digit Modicon number (3xxxxx with function 04, 4xxxxx with function 03) and the hex physical address used in the frame; 300001 / 0000h is wire address 0. Functions 03 and 04 are equivalent.

What byte order does the Carlo Gavazzi EM580 three-phase AC energy meter for EV chargers (Modbus RTU) use for 32-bit values?

Multi-register values in this map use CDAB (low word first (word-swapped)). Set your client to this order for every 32/64-bit value in the table.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Carlo Gavazzi EM580 three-phase AC energy meter for EV chargers (Modbus RTU)?

Start with IR 0 V L1-N (V L1-N); IR 12 A L1 (A L1); IR 40 W sys (W sys); IR 49 PF sys (PF sys); IR 51 Frequency (Frequency); IR 52 kWh (+) TOT (Imported kWh total); IR 78 kWh (-) TOT (Exported kWh total); HR 28741 Charging status (Charging session status).

How do I enable Modbus control of the Carlo Gavazzi EM580 three-phase AC energy meter for EV chargers (Modbus RTU)?

Modbus RTU on the RS485 port is always active; set address and line settings at 2000h-2004h and write 1 to 2010h to apply.
