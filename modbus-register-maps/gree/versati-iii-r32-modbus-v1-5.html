Updated 19 November 2026

Short answer

The Versati wired controller has a two-wire RS-485 BMS port that answers Modbus RTU, so up to 255 heat pumps can sit on one line. Holding registers (words) carry on/off, mode, water and room setpoints, temperature limits, power and current limits, unit status and compressor frequency; coils (bits) carry function switches (timers, quiet mode, weather compensation, disinfection, holiday) and indoor, outdoor and inverter fault and state flags.

This page lists 142 Modbus registers for the Gree Versati heat pump water heater (wired-controller Modbus RTU, protocol V1.5) — 63 writable — with the table, 0-based wire address, data type, word order, scale, units and access of each, re-typed from Gree’s own free documentation (linked below) and checked against it.

Integration notes: Gree Versati heat pump water heater (wired-controller Modbus RTU, protocol V1.5)

Integration in brief

Wire the BMS port of the wired controller to RS-485, set a unique address, then read words 117-143 and coils 64-191 for status and faults, and write word 42 (0xAA/0x55), word 2 (mode) and the setpoint words.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

On the wired controller press COMMISSION, then FUNCTION, page to 'Address', set the BMS address and press OK. The address must not be 0 or 126 and must match the address the BMS sends.

Byte order & addressing

  • Read with function 0x03 and write with 0x10 (words); read with 0x01 and write with 0x0F (bits). Single-write functions 0x05/0x06 are not listed.
  • At most 127 words per read.
  • Broadcast (address 0) is accepted for writes only.
  • BMS and Gree long-distance monitoring have equal control; the most recent command wins.

Network setup

  • 9600 bit/s, 8 data bits, no parity, 1 stop bit.
  • Controller BMS terminal: 1 = B, 2 = A (two-wire, twisted pair).

Watch out for

  • Change the mode only while the unit is off; mode writes while running may be ignored.
  • Hot-water related modes and functions are ignored when no water tank is configured, and cooling modes are ignored on heating-only units.
  • After commanding disinfection, check word 135 for its state.

Notes on this map

  • Gree does not appear to host this protocol publicly; the link is an unaltered copy of Gree's document in the Ukrainian distributor Kiev Climate's manual library, filed there under Versati III as 'R32_Versati Modbus V1.5'. Page headers inside still read 'V1.1' while the cover says V1.5.
  • Every word is printed as an unsigned integer with transmission value = actual value. Words whose printed range goes below 0 °C are left out because the document gives no rule for coding negative values: settings 14 (T-Eheater), 15 (T-Other switch on) and 18 (Lower AT-Heat), and all sensor readings 118-131 (outdoor, discharge, defrost, suction, economizer, discharge pressure, water out/in, optional water sensor, tank, remote room, gas pipe and liquid pipe temperatures).
  • Reserved words and bits are left out, as are bits 99, 100, 177, 178 and 184, which are named but have no access type printed.
  • Word 42 uses 0xAA for on and 0x55 for off, not 1/0. Word 43 is sent as kW x 10.
  • Several limits differ between high-temperature and low-temperature units (words 10 and 21); the description quotes both ranges.

Check it against the live device. A register map tells you what the controller can expose; the unit in front of you may differ by firmware or options. Read a few registers and compare them with the unit’s own display; for a 32-bit value the float and byte-order decoder shows all four word orders at once, so a wrong word order or scale is obvious. Questions about the Gree Versati heat pump water heater (wired-controller Modbus RTU, protocol V1.5)

What Modbus registers does the Gree Versati heat pump water heater (wired-controller Modbus RTU, protocol V1.5) have?

This map lists 142 registers: 93 coils, 49 holding registers. 63 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Gree Versati heat pump water heater (wired-controller Modbus RTU, protocol V1.5) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers 0-based (the number printed is the address on the wire). Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. Registers are words numbered from 0 and coils are bits numbered from 0 (Bit 0 = Byte0.0). Word n is holding register n and Bit n is coil n on the wire; the protocol's example frames agree (read from word 1 sends 00 01, read from coil 5 sends 00 05).

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Gree Versati heat pump water heater (wired-controller Modbus RTU, protocol V1.5)?

Start with HR 42 On/Off (On/off); HR 2 Mode (Mode); HR 10 WOT-Heat (Heating water setpoint); HR 13 T-water tank (Tank setpoint); HR 117 Unit status (Unit status); HR 143 Running frequency (Compressor running frequency); CO 80 Compressor State (Compressor on); CO 86 Defrosting State (Defrosting); CO 102 System Recoverable Protection (System recoverable protection).

How do I enable Modbus control of the Gree Versati heat pump water heater (wired-controller Modbus RTU, protocol V1.5)?

On the wired controller press COMMISSION, then FUNCTION, page to 'Address', set the BMS address and press OK. The address must not be 0 or 126 and must match the address the BMS sends.
