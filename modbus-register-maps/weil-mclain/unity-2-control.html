Short answer

Weil-McLain's Unity 2 boiler control has a built-in Modbus RTU slave on the BMS terminals of J12 (a plug-in harness on ECO Tec, pre-installed board on SVF). This map covers the supplement's 'frequently used points': model, boiler state, active priority, blower speed and modulation, flame signal, all temperature sensors in degrees F, DHW flow, gas valve and 120 V output status, and gas type.

Integration notes: Weil-McLain Unity 2 control (ECO Tec 80-199, SVF 500/600 boilers)

Integration in brief

Enable Modbus on the boiler's contractor menu, match address/baud/parity/stop bits, then read 0xA002-0xA01E with function 03 for state, firing and temperatures.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

Contractor menu (press and hold the WM logo on the home screen) > Modbus settings: set Enable = YES, address, parity, baud and stop bits. BMS wiring is J12-1 (A+), J12-2 (B-), J12-5 (common).

Byte order & addressing

  • Register numbers like 40962 are real addresses (0xA002), not Modicon references.
  • Temperatures in F with fault codes 1233-1237.

Network setup

  • Baud 4800, 9600 or 38400; parity even/odd/none; 1 or 2 stop bits.

Watch out for

  • On multi-boiler systems the Unity 2 must be the master control.

Notes on this map

  • Several registers are typed UINT8 in the list; Modbus returns them as a 16-bit register and they are typed UINT16 here.
  • Temperatures are whole degrees F; the values 1233-1237 are sensor fault codes, not temperatures.
  • Only the frequently used points list and gas type are mapped. The 118-page supplement also covers priorities, network/shadow boilers, faults and history; those registers are left out to keep the map short.
  • Modbus settings (enable, address 0-255, parity even/odd/none, baud 4800/9600/38400, 1 or 2 stop bits) are made on the boiler display's Modbus settings screen; no defaults are given.
  • The supplement does not name Modbus function codes. Its single register list mixes read-only and user-writable registers, which fits the holding-register table, so the points are listed as holding registers (function 03).
  • Revision date taken from the part number suffix /1023 (October 2023).

Check it against the live device. A register map tells you what the controller can expose; the unit in front of you may differ by firmware or options. Read a few registers and compare them with the unit’s own display; for a 32-bit value the float and byte-order decoder shows all four word orders at once, so a wrong word order or scale is obvious. Questions about the Weil-McLain Unity 2 control (ECO Tec 80-199, SVF 500/600 boilers)

What Modbus registers does the Weil-McLain Unity 2 control (ECO Tec 80-199, SVF 500/600 boilers) have?

This map lists 30 registers: 30 holding registers. 0 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Weil-McLain Unity 2 control (ECO Tec 80-199, SVF 500/600 boilers) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers hexadecimal, 0-based. Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. The supplement lists each register in decimal and hex and says the control speaks hex: use the hex value as the register address. 0xA002 (decimal 40962) is sent as 0xA002; it is not a Modicon 4xxxx number, so do not subtract 40001.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Weil-McLain Unity 2 control (ECO Tec 80-199, SVF 500/600 boilers)?

Start with HR 40962 Boiler state (Boiler state); HR 40967 Modulation percentage (Modulation %); HR 40970 Target temperature (Target temperature); HR 40971 Boiler out 1 temperature (Boiler outlet temperature); HR 40973 Boiler in temperature (Boiler inlet temperature); HR 40978 Outdoor temperature (Outdoor temperature).

How do I enable Modbus control of the Weil-McLain Unity 2 control (ECO Tec 80-199, SVF 500/600 boilers)?

Contractor menu (press and hold the WM logo on the home screen) > Modbus settings: set Enable = YES, address, parity, baud and stop bits. BMS wiring is J12-1 (A+), J12-2 (B-), J12-5 (common).
