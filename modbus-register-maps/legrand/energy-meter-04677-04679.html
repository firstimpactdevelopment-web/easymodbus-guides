Short answer

Legrand's 046 77 and 046 79 are two-module DIN-rail energy meters with an RS485 port speaking Modbus/JBUS. The protocol sheet lists a small set of words: voltage, current, active power with a separate sign word, power factor with its inductive/capacitive sector, frequency, total and partial active energy, run time, a reset word and a device identifier.

Integration notes: Legrand Energy meter 046 77 / 046 79 (2-module, RS485 Modbus)

Integration in brief

Read holding registers with function 03 from 0x2000: 16 words cover voltage, current, power with its sign word, power factor, frequency, total and partial energy and run time.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

Set the meter's Modbus address and baud rate on the device; the protocol sheet does not describe the menu.

Byte order & addressing

  • Power is unsigned with a separate sign word (0x2006).
  • Voltage and current come in mV and mA.
  • Hex addresses are wire addresses.

Network setup

  • 8 data bits, 1 stop bit; baud rate programmable.
  • Up to 32 devices per segment without repeaters.

Watch out for

  • The sheet lists only function 03 for reading and 0x10 for writing.
  • The reset word 0x00C8 is write-only.

Notes on this map

  • Data format (2.2): a 'long' is two words sent most significant word first, so 32-bit values are ABCD; the example answer 00 00 64 8C reads as 25740.
  • The sheet calls the 32-bit format just 'long' without saying signed or unsigned. Power and power factor are magnitudes with separate sign/sector words and energies only count up, so the values are listed as unsigned; any reading below 2^31 decodes the same either way.
  • Only functions 0x03 (read words) and 0x10 (write words) are supported. Address 0 is broadcast with no answer.
  • The sheet's example labels the second value read from 0x200A as 'total reactive energy', but the variables table puts positive partial active energy at 0x200C; the table is followed here.
  • Baud rate is programmable on the meter; the sheet gives 8 data bits and 1 stop bit but no parity or default speed.
  • Timing: keep gaps between characters under 100 ms; the meter answers no sooner than 25 ms.

Check it against the live device. A register map tells you what the controller can expose; the unit in front of you may differ by firmware or options. Read a few registers and compare them with the unit’s own display; for a 32-bit value the float and byte-order decoder shows all four word orders at once, so a wrong word order or scale is obvious. Questions about the Legrand Energy meter 046 77 / 046 79 (2-module, RS485 Modbus)

What Modbus registers does the Legrand Energy meter 046 77 / 046 79 (2-module, RS485 Modbus) have?

This map lists 12 registers: 12 holding registers. 1 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Legrand Energy meter 046 77 / 046 79 (2-module, RS485 Modbus) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers hexadecimal, 0-based. Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. Addresses are printed in hex as sent in the request. The sheet's example reads 4 words starting at 0x200A with the frame 01 03 20 0A 00 04, and writes the reset word with 01 10 00 C8 00 01 02 00 01.

What byte order does the Legrand Energy meter 046 77 / 046 79 (2-module, RS485 Modbus) use for 32-bit values?

Multi-register values in this map use ABCD (high word first (big-endian)). Set your client to this order for every 32/64-bit value in the table.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Legrand Energy meter 046 77 / 046 79 (2-module, RS485 Modbus)?

Start with HR 8202 Positive active energy (total) (Total positive active energy (0.01 kWh)); HR 8192 Voltage (Voltage (mV)); HR 8194 Current (Current (mA)); HR 8196 Active power (magnitude) (Active power magnitude); HR 8198 Active power sign (Active power sign); HR 8201 Frequency (Frequency).

How do I enable Modbus control of the Legrand Energy meter 046 77 / 046 79 (2-module, RS485 Modbus)?

Set the meter's Modbus address and baud rate on the device; the protocol sheet does not describe the menu.
