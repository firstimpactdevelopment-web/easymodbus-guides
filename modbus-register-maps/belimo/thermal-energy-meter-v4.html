Integration notes: Belimo Thermal Energy Meter (TEM) DN 15-50, Version 4 (Modbus RTU / TCP)

Integration in brief

Poll Nos. 20-24 (temperatures and delta T), 8 (flow), 28/34 (cooling/heating power) and 66/72 (energy) with FC03, decoding 32-bit values low word first. Check No. 105 for sensor and flow faults.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

Modbus is the native interface; set address, baud rate and frame format with Belimo Assistant 2 or the built-in web server.

Byte order & addressing

  • 32-bit values are low word first (CDAB).
  • Register No. vs Address: subtract 1 for the wire address.
  • On MID models (22PEM) register 205 picks certified vs lifetime counters; switching it changes what the energy registers show.

Network setup

  • RTU defaults: address 1, 38400 baud, 8-N-2; also 8-N-1, 8-E-1, 8-O-1 and 9600-115200 baud.
  • Up to 32 nodes without repeater; internal 120 ohm terminator set by tool.

Watch out for

  • The certified meter register resets when the sensor module is replaced; avoid toggling register 205, as it disturbs data logging.

Notes on this map

  • Word order: the manual says 32-bit values use two consecutive registers with the least significant word first (register No. x = low word, x+1 = high word); both must be written together with FC16. Mapped as CDAB.
  • Values are unsigned unless marked signed; the signed ones are the sensor 1 temperature and temperature 1/2 registers. Unsupported registers read 65535.
  • Left out: flow, power, volume and nominal-flow/power registers 'in selected unit' (Nos. 10/11, 32/33, 38/39, 64/65, 113/114, 164/165), because the printed scale applies to several units at once and does not map cleanly to every unit choice. Energy in selected unit (70/71, 76/77) is listed since its scale is 1 for every unit.
  • The power-cooling-in-selected-unit row is printed as 'No. 32, Address 30' in the description table but 'No. 32, Address 31' in the overview; it is omitted anyway.
  • Registers above No. 100 that are writable are stored permanently; do not write them cyclically.
  • The PDF was downloaded for checking from a distributor mirror (zotsupply.com) that carries the same edition; the link above is Belimo's own copy.

Check it against the live device. A register map tells you what the controller can expose; the unit in front of you may differ by firmware or options. Read a few registers and compare them with the unit’s own display; for a 32-bit value the float and byte-order decoder shows all four word orders at once, so a wrong word order or scale is obvious. Questions about the Belimo Thermal Energy Meter (TEM) DN 15-50, Version 4 (Modbus RTU / TCP)

What Modbus registers does the Belimo Thermal Energy Meter (TEM) DN 15-50, Version 4 (Modbus RTU / TCP) have?

This map lists 45 registers: 45 holding registers. 7 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Belimo Thermal Energy Meter (TEM) DN 15-50, Version 4 (Modbus RTU / TCP) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers 1-based (subtract 1 for the wire address). Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. Belimo lists 'No.' (from 1) and 'Address' (from 0) for each register; 'documented' is the No. column, so No. 8 is wire address 7. All data sit in one table, reachable with FC03 reads and FC06/FC16 writes; FC04 and FC02 also work as alternatives.

What byte order does the Belimo Thermal Energy Meter (TEM) DN 15-50, Version 4 (Modbus RTU / TCP) use for 32-bit values?

Multi-register values in this map use CDAB (low word first (word-swapped)). Set your client to this order for every 32/64-bit value in the table.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Belimo Thermal Energy Meter (TEM) DN 15-50, Version 4 (Modbus RTU / TCP)?

Start with HR 7 Absolute volumetric flow (Flow l/s); HR 19 Temperature 1 (remote) (Remote temperature); HR 21 Temperature 2 (flow body) (Flow-body temperature); HR 23 Delta temperature (Delta T); HR 27 Absolute power cooling (Cooling power kW); HR 33 Absolute power heating (Heating power kW); HR 65 Absolute energy cooling (Cooling energy kWh); HR 71 Absolute energy heating (Heating energy kWh); HR 104 Malfunction and service information (Fault bitmask).

How do I enable Modbus control of the Belimo Thermal Energy Meter (TEM) DN 15-50, Version 4 (Modbus RTU / TCP)?

Modbus is the native interface; set address, baud rate and frame format with Belimo Assistant 2 or the built-in web server.
