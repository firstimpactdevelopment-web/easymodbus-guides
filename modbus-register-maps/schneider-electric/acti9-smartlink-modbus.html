Short answer

The Acti 9 Smartlink gathers status, commands and pulse counts from up to 11 Ti24 channels (iOF+SD24 auxiliaries, iACT24/iATL24, Reflex iC60, RCA iC60, pulse meters such as iEM2000T) and serves them on one Modbus RTU address. Summary blocks give all channels in one read; each channel also has a 40-register detail block. 32-bit counters are big-endian.

This page lists 80 Modbus registers for the Schneider Electric Acti 9 Smartlink Modbus (A9XMSB11) 11-channel I/O and pulse concentrator — 31 writable — with the table, 0-based wire address, data type, word order, scale, units and access of each, re-typed from Schneider Electric’s own free documentation (linked below) and checked against it.

Integration notes: Schneider Electric Acti 9 Smartlink Modbus (A9XMSB11) 11-channel I/O and pulse concentrator

Integration in brief

Read 120-121 for all input states, 14050-14093 for every channel's energy/pulse totals and 14000-14043 for rates; write bits in 130/131 to open or close Acti 9 devices.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

Set the Modbus address (01-99) on the two thumbwheels. Speed, parity and stop bits are picked up from the master automatically at power-up.

Byte order & addressing

  • Addresses are protocol (0-based) addresses.
  • Order bits are cleared by the Smartlink once executed.
  • In a channel order register (14201 / 14202), setting bit 0 and bit 1 together has no effect.

Network setup

  • Thumbwheels: left = tens, right = units (01-99).
  • Default 19200 baud, even parity, 1 stop bit; 4800 and 9600 also supported.
  • Factory reset: power off, set address 00, power on.

Watch out for

  • Auto-adaptation only happens at power-up.
  • Pulse weights default to 10; set 14230/14231 per channel to match the meter.

Notes on this map

  • The manual states 32-bit values are big-endian: most significant word first.
  • Channel n detail registers = channel 1 register + 40 x (n-1) (14200, 14240 ... 14600). Only channel 1's detail block is listed here; the summary blocks 14000-14165 cover all channels.
  • Power/flow and consumption units depend on the meter on that input (Wh, m3, L ...), set by its pulse weight. The flow value drops to 0 when pulses stop.
  • Left out: identification strings (100, 109), date/time (115) and counter setting dates (DATE type), and the embedded configuration blocks beyond the channel 1 product type.
  • The Smartlink adapts to the master's baud and parity after up to 25 frames at power-up; with no parity it needs 2 stop bits.

Check it against the live device. A register map tells you what the controller can expose; the unit in front of you may differ by firmware or options. Read a few registers and compare them with the unit’s own display; for a 32-bit value the float and byte-order decoder shows all four word orders at once, so a wrong word order or scale is obvious. Questions about the Schneider Electric Acti 9 Smartlink Modbus (A9XMSB11) 11-channel I/O and pulse concentrator

What Modbus registers does the Schneider Electric Acti 9 Smartlink Modbus (A9XMSB11) 11-channel I/O and pulse concentrator have?

This map lists 80 registers: 80 holding registers. 31 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Schneider Electric Acti 9 Smartlink Modbus (A9XMSB11) 11-channel I/O and pulse concentrator Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers 0-based (the number printed is the address on the wire). Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. The manual prints Modbus protocol addresses, which start at 0, and says a master that uses data-model numbering must add 1. Its frame example reads address 14050 as 0x36E2.

What byte order does the Schneider Electric Acti 9 Smartlink Modbus (A9XMSB11) 11-channel I/O and pulse concentrator use for 32-bit values?

Multi-register values in this map use ABCD (high word first (big-endian)). Set your client to this order for every 32/64-bit value in the table.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Schneider Electric Acti 9 Smartlink Modbus (A9XMSB11) 11-channel I/O and pulse concentrator?

Start with HR 112 Smartlink status and diagnostics (Module health); HR 120 Input 1 state, all channels (Input 1 state of all channels); HR 121 Input 2 state, all channels (Input 2 state of all channels); HR 130 Open order, Acti 9 products (Open orders); HR 131 Close order, Acti 9 products (Close orders); HR 14050 Ch 1 I1 consumption meter (Channel 1 meter total).

How do I enable Modbus control of the Schneider Electric Acti 9 Smartlink Modbus (A9XMSB11) 11-channel I/O and pulse concentrator?

Set the Modbus address (01-99) on the two thumbwheels. Speed, parity and stop bits are picked up from the master automatically at power-up.
