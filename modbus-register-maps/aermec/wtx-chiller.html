Integration notes: Aermec WTX water-cooled chiller with Turbocor compressors (pCO5+ BMS)

Integration in brief

Read coil 1 for unit on, coil 100 (sum of all alarms) and integer 5206 for unit status; write coil 10 to switch the plant from the BMS and coil 2 to reset alarms. Demand limit is integer 5012 (R/W).

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

Fit the AER485P1 card (RS485) or AERNET (Ethernet) in the pCO5+ BMS slot; BMS card defaults are 19200 baud, no parity, 2 stop bits.

Byte order & addressing

  • Addresses are protocol (base 0) addresses; do not subtract 1.
  • Integers live at 5001 + Carel index; digitals and analogues use the plain index.

Network setup

  • Set the BMS address and serial settings on the pCO5+ to match the master (default 19200 baud, 8N2 as listed).

Watch out for

  • Without a stated scale, verify any analogue value you add against the unit display before using it.

Notes on this map

  • Left out: all analogue variables (temperatures, pressures, setpoints, addresses 1-205). The manual lists ranges like -999.9 to 999.9 but never states the register scale, so the raw-to-value factor is not settled.
  • Also left out: compressor speed, minimum and maximum speed registers (5006/5007, 5037-5040, 5104/5105, 5116-5119), because the '[0 ... 48000] / 10 RPM' wording does not make clear whether the register holds rpm or rpm/10; and 5003 (Aermec internal use).
  • Run-hour counters come in two parts: an upper register in thousands of hours and a lower register in hours (0-999).
  • Digital points are read with function 1 and written with function 5, so they are listed as coils; integer variables use functions 3 and 6.

Questions about the Aermec WTX water-cooled chiller with Turbocor compressors (pCO5+ BMS)

What Modbus registers does the Aermec WTX water-cooled chiller with Turbocor compressors (pCO5+ BMS) have?

This map lists 293 registers: 239 coils, 54 holding registers. 4 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Aermec WTX water-cooled chiller with Turbocor compressors (pCO5+ BMS) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers 0-based (the number printed is the address on the wire). Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. The manual states protocol addresses are base 0 and that addresses start from zero; its example reads address 1 with the frame 01 03 00 01. The BMS addresses in the tables go on the wire unchanged. Integer variables sit at 5001 + the Carel index (Carel 1 = 5002).

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Aermec WTX water-cooled chiller with Turbocor compressors (pCO5+ BMS)?

Start with CO 1 On / Off Unit (Unit on/off status); CO 10 On / Off plant by supervisor (Plant on/off by BMS); CO 2 Reset alarms (Alarm reset); HR 5206 Units Status (Unit status); HR 5012 Cold demand limit (Cooling demand limit %); HR 5025 Total plant request (Total plant request %); HR 5002 Chiller Mode (Chiller mode); CO 100 Sum of all alarms (Sum of all alarms).

How do I enable Modbus control of the Aermec WTX water-cooled chiller with Turbocor compressors (pCO5+ BMS)?

Fit the AER485P1 card (RS485) or AERNET (Ethernet) in the pCO5+ BMS slot; BMS card defaults are 19200 baud, no parity, 2 stop bits.
