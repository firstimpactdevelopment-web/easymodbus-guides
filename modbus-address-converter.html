Modbus address converter

Free browser tool · nothing is uploaded · updated 18 September 2026

The same Modbus register is written four different ways, and the number that actually travels on the wire is the zero-based one — which is why values so often come out one slot off. Type an address in whatever form your manual uses and this converter shows the table, the protocol (wire) address, the function codes, and every notation. Background: why is my Modbus address off by one?

Try 40007, 4x00007, 30001, 00001, or a plain number like 6.
