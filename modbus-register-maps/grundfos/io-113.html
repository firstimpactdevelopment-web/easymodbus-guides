Short answer

The IO 113 supervises a submersible sewage pump: winding and bearing temperatures, insulation resistance, vibration, water in oil, moisture and PTC switches, run hours and starts. With DIP switch 9 on it answers as a Modbus RTU slave. This map covers the measured and peak values, alarm and warning codes and bits, the command registers, the alarm limits and the counters.

Integration notes: Grundfos IO 113 pump protection module (Modbus RTU)

Integration in brief

Set DIP switch 9 on for Modbus, choose the address with SW3-SW5 (or 231 default), and poll 1992-2021 with function 03 at 19200 8E1. Read alarm_code/warning_code (2010/2011) when supervise_mode_status is not zero, and write a non-zero value to RESET_ALARM (2033) to clear a pending alarm.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

DIP switch 9 = on selects Modbus instead of GENIbus. Commands such as START, STOP, RESET_ALARM and RESET_HIST are executed by writing a non-zero value to their register.

Byte order & addressing

  • Register numbers are the wire addresses; do not subtract 1.
  • Older PC Tool WU versions (before TC06.00.06) show unit numbers offset by 31 from the real address.
  • FACT_BOOT and USER_BOOT only take effect after a RESET command or a power cycle; every other setting applies at once.

Network setup

  • Fixed format 19200 baud, 8 data bits, even parity, 1 stop bit.
  • Address range 32-231 (factory 231). SW3-SW5 select 40-45; with them all off the address comes from unit_addr (2050).

Watch out for

  • Keep request and reply under 80 bytes, so read at most about 37 registers per request.
  • Write no more than two configuration parameters per telegram.

Notes on this map

  • Only function 03 is supported for reading (plus 06, 16 and 08 sub-function 0). Telegrams may not exceed 80 bytes and at most 4 registers can be written per telegram; only two configuration parameters can be written per telegram because they are forwarded to the SM 113 over the power line.
  • Counters are split into _hi and _lo registers with _hi at the lower address, so they are listed as ABCD 32-bit values.
  • A 16-bit value of 0x00FF means not available unless stated otherwise.
  • Items marked for unit type 2 (power-line communication with SM 113) are only valid with the SM 113 sensor board: bearing temperatures, insulation resistance and vibration.
  • Vibration values use a scaling of 20 mm/s per 254 counts (about 0.0787 mm/s per count).
  • Left out: wio_output_offset 2056 (no scaling or access printed) and sensors_present_set 2062 (write-only preset with no value list). Front LED bit fields are long multi-bit codes and are not decoded here.

Check it against the live device. A register map tells you what the controller can expose; the unit in front of you may differ by firmware or options. Read a few registers and compare them with the unit’s own display; for a 32-bit value the float and byte-order decoder shows all four word orders at once, so a wrong word order or scale is obvious. Questions about the Grundfos IO 113 pump protection module (Modbus RTU)

What Modbus registers does the Grundfos IO 113 pump protection module (Modbus RTU) have?

This map lists 72 registers: 72 holding registers. 28 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Grundfos IO 113 pump protection module (Modbus RTU) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers 0-based (the number printed is the address on the wire). Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. Register numbers are printed in decimal and used directly as the telegram address: the read example requests 0x07C8 (1992, t_support_bear) and the START example writes 0x07F5 (2037).

What byte order does the Grundfos IO 113 pump protection module (Modbus RTU) use for 32-bit values?

Multi-register values in this map use ABCD (high word first (big-endian)). Set your client to this order for every 32/64-bit value in the table.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Grundfos IO 113 pump protection module (Modbus RTU)?

Start with HR 1994 t_pt100 (Stator temperature (PT100)); HR 1998 water_in_oil (Water in oil); HR 2009 supervise_mode_status (Normal / warning / alarm state); HR 2010 alarm_code (Alarm code); HR 2011 warning_code (Warning code); HR 2012 alarms (Alarm bits); HR 2022 t_run (Running hours (minutes)); HR 2033 RESET_ALARM (Reset alarm command).

How do I enable Modbus control of the Grundfos IO 113 pump protection module (Modbus RTU)?

DIP switch 9 = on selects Modbus instead of GENIbus. Commands such as START, STOP, RESET_ALARM and RESET_HIST are executed by writing a non-zero value to their register.
