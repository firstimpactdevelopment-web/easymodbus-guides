Short answer

Legrand's 4 120 68 is a meter with an RS-485 Modbus RTU port. Its short Modbus table gives voltage, current, frequency, active/reactive/apparent power and power factor as 16-bit words, two 32-bit energy counters, the internal temperature, a date/time block and the baud-rate and address settings. The energy counters are left out of this map because the table does not say which word comes first.

Integration notes: Legrand Energy meter 4 120 68 (RS-485 Modbus)

Integration in brief

Read holding registers with function 03 from address 0: 7 words give voltage, current, frequency, active/reactive/apparent power and power factor. Address 37 is the internal temperature.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

Modbus RTU is active on the RS-485 port; baud rate (register 42) and address (register 43) can be written with function 16, e.g. ADDR 10 002A 0001 02 0002 sets 2400 baud.

Byte order & addressing

  • Register numbers in the table are address + 1; send the address.
  • Power values are unsigned 16-bit.

Network setup

  • Baud codes: 1 = 1200, 2 = 2400, 3 = 4800, 4 = 9600.

Watch out for

  • Active power is 16-bit whole watts, so it tops out at 65535 W.

Notes on this map

  • Left out: positive active energy (address 7, 2 words, x10 Wh) and positive reactive energy (address 17, 2 words, x10 varh). The table only says data are 'Big Endian' (most significant byte first), which fixes the byte order inside a word but not which of the two words is sent first.
  • Supported functions: 03 (read) and 16 (write). Exception codes 1, 2 and 3.
  • Serial details: RS-485 two-wire, RTU, address 1-247, programmable baud rate, 8 data bits, no parity, 1 stop bit.
  • Timing: inter-character time-out 25 ms, response delay 25-100 ms, wait more than 25 ms between master requests.
  • Left out: the 4-register date/day-of-week/time block at 33 (packed BCD-style digits per the example).
  • The table heads the measurement page 'Input registers' but gives read function code 3, so they are listed here as holding registers (function 03).

Questions about the Legrand Energy meter 4 120 68 (RS-485 Modbus)

What Modbus registers does the Legrand Energy meter 4 120 68 (RS-485 Modbus) have?

This map lists 10 registers: 10 holding registers. 2 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Legrand Energy meter 4 120 68 (RS-485 Modbus) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers 0-based (the number printed is the address on the wire). Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. The table gives a 1-based register number and the 0-based register address in decimal and hex (voltage: number 1, address 0). The address is sent on the wire: the date/time example reads register number 34 with the frame ADDR 03 0021 0004.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Legrand Energy meter 4 120 68 (RS-485 Modbus)?

Start with HR 0 Voltage (Voltage); HR 1 Current (Current); HR 3 Active power (Active power); HR 2 Frequency (Frequency); HR 6 Power factor (Power factor).

How do I enable Modbus control of the Legrand Energy meter 4 120 68 (RS-485 Modbus)?

Modbus RTU is active on the RS-485 port; baud rate (register 42) and address (register 43) can be written with function 16, e.g. ADDR 10 002A 0001 02 0002 sets 2400 baud.
