Short answer

The CZ-NSMB is Panasonic's own Modbus RTU slave board that plugs into the CN-CNT connector of an Aquarea air-to-water unit. All points are holding registers: on/off, operating mode, zone and tank setpoints, compensation curves, smart-grid settings, quiet and powerful modes, sterilisation and system reset requests, plus readings of outdoor, water, tank and zone temperatures, error codes, energy use and generation, and unit configuration.

This page lists 153 Modbus registers for the Panasonic CZ-NSMB A2W Modbus PCB for Aquarea air-to-water heat pumps — 75 writable — with the table, 0-based wire address, data type, word order, scale, units and access of each, re-typed from Panasonic’s own free documentation (linked below) and checked against it.

Integration notes: Panasonic CZ-NSMB A2W Modbus PCB for Aquarea air-to-water heat pumps

Integration in brief

Fit the PCB on the CN-CNT connector, set address and baud with SW1, then poll holding registers 0-52 for state and temperatures and write 0 (on/off), 4 (mode), 12/13 or 226-229 (zone setpoints) and 30/33 (tank).

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

No software enable: connect the supplied CN-CNT cable to the unit's main (or optional) PCB, connect CN2 to the RS-485 bus and set the slave address and baud rate on DIP SW1.

Byte order & addressing

  • Serial format is 8N2 by default; 8N1 is also supported.
  • Supported functions: 3, 4, 6 and 16; all points are holding registers.
  • Mode codes 8-11 (Auto Heat, Auto Heat Tank, Auto Cool, Auto Cool Tank) are read-only states.

Network setup

  • DIP SW1 sets slave address (default 1) and baud rate 9600 (default), 19200, 57600 or 115200.
  • DIP SW2 P8 switches in the 120 ohm termination (default off).
  • Up to 63 CZ-NSMB boards per bus; one heat pump per board.
  • On Big A2W outdoor units the PCB sits in the C-BOX and the bus lands on terminals U1/U2.

Watch out for

  • LED D3 flashing means the heat pump has not finished initialising; registers may not be valid yet.
  • The remote controller and Modbus can be used at the same time.

Notes on this map

  • The manual gives no data type per register. Values whose printed range goes below zero (temperatures, water-shift setpoints, curve outdoor limits, tank re-heat and cool capacity shifts) are listed as INT16; all others as UINT16.
  • Temperatures are whole degrees Celsius by default. DIP SW2 P1 ON switches all temperature registers to tenths (x10) and P2 ON to Fahrenheit; the scale of 1 here matches the default.
  • 32-bit totals at 120/121-130/131 and 366/367-372/373 are left out: the manual marks them only '(MSB)' or not at all and never says which register holds the high word. 184/185 (compressor hours) is printed as [MSB/LSB] and is listed as UINT32 with the high word first.
  • Many registers apply only to some series ('If K series onwards', 'If M series'); the condition is quoted in the description. 213 and 437 apply to Big A2W variants.
  • Error code 52 encodes the remote-controller code: 1XX = Hxx, 2XX = Fxx, 3XX = Uxx (e.g. 214 = F14), per the manual's error-code section (the register table itself prints 1XXX/2XXX/3XXX).
  • Write 1337 to 378 to clear the consumption counters.

Check it against the live device. A register map tells you what the controller can expose; the unit in front of you may differ by firmware or options. Read a few registers and compare them with the unit’s own display; for a 32-bit value the float and byte-order decoder shows all four word orders at once, so a wrong word order or scale is obvious. Questions about the Panasonic CZ-NSMB A2W Modbus PCB for Aquarea air-to-water heat pumps

What Modbus registers does the Panasonic CZ-NSMB A2W Modbus PCB for Aquarea air-to-water heat pumps have?

This map lists 153 registers: 153 holding registers. 75 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Panasonic CZ-NSMB A2W Modbus PCB for Aquarea air-to-water heat pumps Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers 0-based (the number printed is the address on the wire). Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. Registers are listed by protocol address starting at 0 (address 0 = Operation on/off), so the printed address goes on the wire unchanged.

What byte order does the Panasonic CZ-NSMB A2W Modbus PCB for Aquarea air-to-water heat pumps use for 32-bit values?

Multi-register values in this map use ABCD (high word first (big-endian)). Set your client to this order for every 32/64-bit value in the table.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Panasonic CZ-NSMB A2W Modbus PCB for Aquarea air-to-water heat pumps?

How do I enable Modbus control of the Panasonic CZ-NSMB A2W Modbus PCB for Aquarea air-to-water heat pumps?
