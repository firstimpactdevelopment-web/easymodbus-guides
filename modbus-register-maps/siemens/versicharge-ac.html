Short answer

The Siemens VersiCharge AC wallbox has a Modbus server on both RS-485 (RTU) and Ethernet (TCP) once Modbus is enabled for the charger in the cloud configuration. Everything is a holding register. This map covers identity and hardware data, the J1772 and OCPP state, error codes, per-phase current, voltage, power, power factor and energy, the Modbus charging-current limit with its fallback current and timeout and the reset/address registers.

This page lists 53 Modbus registers for the Siemens VersiCharge AC series wallbox (Modbus map) — 7 writable — with the table, 0-based wire address, data type, word order, scale, units and access of each, re-typed from Siemens’s own free documentation (linked below) and checked against it.

Integration notes: Siemens VersiCharge AC series wallbox (Modbus map)

Integration in brief

Poll internal 1599-1602 for state and faults and 1647-1677 for live values with function 3. To manage load, set the fallback current (1660) and fallback time (1661) once after every reboot, then write the charging current limit to 1633 inside that time window.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

The charger must be commissioned (register 27 shows the link used), and the 'Modbus' setting must be True in the charger's VersiCloud group; otherwise the Modbus application does not start.

Communication loss & watchdog

If Modbus traffic stops for the Fallback Time (1661, 0 or 60-600 s), the charger applies the Fallback Current Phase Sum (1660). Both registers reset at power cycle or Modbus soft reset and must be written again.

Byte order & addressing

  • Registers are documented as 40001-style external numbers next to internal (wire) numbers; use the internal number in the request.
  • Supported functions: 03 read, 06 and 16 write (per register as listed in the map).
  • The charging current limit accepts amps or hundredths of amps depending on the size of the value written, but always reads back in amps.
  • Dynamic values may take several poll cycles to update after a change.

Network setup

  • RTU: 38400 baud, 8 data bits, even parity, 1 stop bit, default address 2 (change at internal 1825, about 20 s to apply; write 1 to 1878 to restore 2).
  • TCP: port 502, fixed, IPv4.
  • Minimum timeout setting 10 s and minimum poll interval 5 s on both transports.

Watch out for

  • A soft reset (write 1 to 1826) restarts the Modbus application, which takes about 60 s to come back.
  • Fallback current and fallback time are not persistent; re-write them after every reboot or soft reset.

Notes on this map

  • The energy counter (1692) is marked 'Uint32 (Big-endian)' in the map, so the high word comes first (ABCD).
  • Charging current limit (internal 1633): written values of 100 or less are whole amps, values 101-8000 are hundredths of an amp (write 6 or 600 for 6 A, 850 for 8.5 A). A read always returns whole amps in the 0-80 range. Values above 8000 are rejected, and anything under 6 A pauses charging. Other limits (OCPP smart charging, cloud power level) still apply on top.
  • Left out: the charging session energy target (internal 1640). Its units column says watt-hours but its description talks about kWh, so the scale cannot be settled from the map.
  • Production date (5-6): only the first word (year) is listed; the second packs month and day as two bytes.
  • Not listed: the five-register firmware strings for the M0 processor, the firmware variant and release-date strings, RFID card add/remove and whitelist blocks (40081-40338, 41853-41878) and the factory-reset salt/command registers, which are marked for internal use.
  • The map warns that when the charger is controlled by OCPP, Modbus is mainly a monitoring interface.

Check it against the live device. A register map tells you what the controller can expose; the unit in front of you may differ by firmware or options. Questions about the Siemens VersiCharge AC series wallbox (Modbus map)

What Modbus registers does the Siemens VersiCharge AC series wallbox (Modbus map) have?

This map lists 53 registers: 53 holding registers. 7 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Siemens VersiCharge AC series wallbox (Modbus map) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers Modicon 3xxxx/4xxxx (drop the prefix, subtract 1). Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. The map prints every register four ways: 'external' decimal (40001 style), 'internal' decimal, and both in hex. External 40001 is internal 0, so the internal number is the wire address: external 41634 = internal 1633 = 0x661. 'documented' below is the external number; 'address' is the internal one. The map's own notes mix the two (it cites both 'register 1633' and 'register 41661'), so check which one is meant.

What byte order does the Siemens VersiCharge AC series wallbox (Modbus map) use for 32-bit values?

Multi-register values in this map use ABCD (high word first (big-endian)). Set your client to this order for every 32/64-bit value in the table.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Siemens VersiCharge AC series wallbox (Modbus map)?

Start with HR 1599 EVSE state (J1772 charging state); HR 1600 EVSE error code (Active fault code); HR 1665 Charging power total (Total charging power); HR 1647 Charging current L1 (Phase L1 current); HR 1692 Real energy consumed (Energy counter); HR 1633 Charging current limit (Charging current limit (write)); HR 1660 Fallback current phase sum (Fallback current); HR 1661 Fallback time (Fallback time).

How do I enable Modbus control of the Siemens VersiCharge AC series wallbox (Modbus map)?

The charger must be commissioned (register 27 shows the link used), and the 'Modbus' setting must be True in the charger's VersiCloud group; otherwise the Modbus application does not start.

What happens when the Siemens VersiCharge AC series wallbox (Modbus map) loses Modbus communication?

If Modbus traffic stops for the Fallback Time (1661, 0 or 60-600 s), the charger applies the Fallback Current Phase Sum (1660). Both registers reset at power cycle or Modbus soft reset and must be written again.
