Short answer

The DTS family are compact DIN-rail, panel and socket energy sub-meters and transducers for single-phase, three-phase and DC circuits. Every value is a signed 32-bit integer in two holding registers, low word first. This map covers voltages, currents, frequency, power, power factor, unbalance, nett and split energy counters, identity and CT/PT ratio registers, and the RS-485 settings.

This page lists 78 Modbus registers for the Measurlogic DTS family energy sub-meters (DTS 101/105/305/310/SMX/SKT/DC) — 8 writable — with the table, 0-based wire address, data type, word order, scale, units and access of each, re-typed from Measurlogic’s own free documentation (linked below) and checked against it.

Integration notes: Measurlogic DTS family energy sub-meters (DTS 101/105/305/310/SMX/SKT/DC)

Integration in brief

Read holding registers in CDAB 32-bit pairs at address 100 (default). Read EnerPowDivider (16045) first and scale power and energy with raw x divider x 0.001.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

Modbus is built in (RS-485 RTU or Ethernet TCP depending on model). Communication parameters and address are normally set with Measurlogic's DTSConfig software.

Byte order & addressing

  • Every value is 32-bit signed, low word first (CDAB).
  • Published addresses are 1-based.
  • Power/energy resolution depends on EnerPowDivider.

Network setup

  • Default slave address 100.
  • RS-485 baud rate 9600 or 19200; change it with DTSConfig rather than by writing registers.
  • Do not change the RS-485 registers on Ethernet models.

Watch out for

  • Writing wrong values to 16119-16123 can make the meter unreachable.
  • Not every register is used on every model or wiring; only connected channels and the Total/Average registers carry valid data.

Notes on this map

  • Measurlogic does not host this map publicly that we could find; this copy is on a distributor's document CDN and is the unaltered Measurlogic document (Measurlogic header and footer on every page).
  • All registers are signed 32-bit values with the LO register at the listed address and the HI register next (CDAB).
  • Power, demand and energy registers have a variable resolution: engineering value in W / VA / var / Wh = raw x EnerPowDivider (register 16045) x 0.001. Their scale is therefore left at 1 and units blank; read 16045 once and apply the formula.
  • Energy registers roll over after 999,999,999 counts.
  • Min/max registers (116xx / 122xx), single-cycle currents, I/O channel status/coils (15301+), the output command register 40001 and the SunSpec map at 50001 are left out.
  • Default Modbus address is 100 (on Modbus TCP it is always 100).
  • Power factor registers (11100-11107) are left out: the manual marks their format 'Special' without defining it.

Check it against the live device. A register map tells you what the controller can expose; the unit in front of you may differ by firmware or options. Read a few registers and compare them with the unit’s own display; for a 32-bit value the float and byte-order decoder shows all four word orders at once, so a wrong word order or scale is obvious. Questions about the Measurlogic DTS family energy sub-meters (DTS 101/105/305/310/SMX/SKT/DC)

What Modbus registers does the Measurlogic DTS family energy sub-meters (DTS 101/105/305/310/SMX/SKT/DC) have?

This map lists 78 registers: 78 holding registers. 8 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Measurlogic DTS family energy sub-meters (DTS 101/105/305/310/SMX/SKT/DC) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers 1-based (subtract 1 for the wire address). Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. Section 2.1.1 says the published addresses are 1-based in the 4x region; subtract 1 for the wire address (Voltage_LN_1 11001 -> 11000). Some masters want the 4 prefix (411001).

What byte order does the Measurlogic DTS family energy sub-meters (DTS 101/105/305/310/SMX/SKT/DC) use for 32-bit values?

Multi-register values in this map use CDAB (low word first (word-swapped)). Set your client to this order for every 32/64-bit value in the table.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Measurlogic DTS family energy sub-meters (DTS 101/105/305/310/SMX/SKT/DC)?

Start with HR 11000 Voltage_LN_1 (Voltage L1-N); HR 11024 Current_1 (Current 1); HR 11054 PowerP_Total (Total active power (raw)); HR 14006 EnergyP_Total (Total nett active energy (raw)); HR 16044 EnerPowDivider (EnerPowDivider).

How do I enable Modbus control of the Measurlogic DTS family energy sub-meters (DTS 101/105/305/310/SMX/SKT/DC)?

Modbus is built in (RS-485 RTU or Ethernet TCP depending on model). Communication parameters and address are normally set with Measurlogic's DTSConfig software.
