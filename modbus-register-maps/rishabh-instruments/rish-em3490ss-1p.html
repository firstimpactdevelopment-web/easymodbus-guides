Short answer

The single-phase RISH EM 3490 SS is a panel energy meter with an optional RS-485 Modbus RTU port. Every measured value and every setting is an IEEE 32-bit float spread over two registers: readings are input registers (FC04) from 30001, settings are holding registers (FC03 read, FC16 write) from 40005. This map covers the full measured-value table, the setup registers and the word-order switch.

Integration notes: Rishabh Instruments RISH EM 3490 SS single-phase energy meter

Integration in brief

Read 30001-30038 and 30071-30082 with FC04 (max 20 floats per request) and decode each pair as a big-endian float. Settings live in 40005-40071 and are written with FC16 as floats.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

Modbus is an ordering option. Set node address, baud/parity (RS485 set-up code) and energy unit from the front panel or via 40019/40021/40005.

Byte order & addressing

  • Settings are floats too: PT secondary 239.6 V is written as 43 6F 99 9A.
  • Reading or writing only part of a float returns exception 02 (illegal data address).
  • 40041 switches the float word order for every register; write 2141.0 in the order you want to use.

Network setup

  • RS485 set-up code (40019) packs baud, parity and stop bits; see the value list.
  • If a password is set, write it to 40071 before changing any 4X register.

Watch out for

  • The energy unit (Wh/kWh/MWh) is a setting, so scale energy values according to 40005.

Notes on this map

  • All registers are 32-bit floats in two consecutive registers. The data-format table states IEEE 754 floats, most significant byte first, and the PT-secondary example reads 43 6F 99 9A for 239.6 V, so ABCD is the default (the voltage example's bytes 43 6E E9 7B decode to 238.9 V, not the 239.6 V printed beside them); 40041 can switch the meter to reversed order.
  • The register table has no unit column, so units are left blank. The manual notes active energy reads in Wh and apparent energy in VAh, while 40005 lets you change the energy unit to kWh or MWh.
  • Up to 20 parameters (40 registers) per read; larger requests return an exception.
  • Left out: the 20 user-assignable registers (30513-30551, mapped through 40513-40534), which mirror any 3X value; see the manual's Tables 9 and 10.
  • Serial defaults are not printed in the manual; baud is selectable 2400-19200 bps (40019 or front panel).

Check it against the live device. A register map tells you what the controller can expose; the unit in front of you may differ by firmware or options. Read a few registers and compare them with the unit’s own display; for a 32-bit value the float and byte-order decoder shows all four word orders at once, so a wrong word order or scale is obvious. Questions about the Rishabh Instruments RISH EM 3490 SS single-phase energy meter

What Modbus registers does the Rishabh Instruments RISH EM 3490 SS single-phase energy meter have?

This map lists 25 registers: 10 input registers, 15 holding registers. 12 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Rishabh Instruments RISH EM 3490 SS single-phase energy meter Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers Modicon 3xxxx/4xxxx (drop the prefix, subtract 1). Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. The manual prints 30001/40001-style register numbers next to the hex start address that goes on the wire (30001 = 00 00, 40011 = 00 0A). Wire address = printed number - 30001 or - 40001; every row was checked against the printed hex.

What byte order does the Rishabh Instruments RISH EM 3490 SS single-phase energy meter use for 32-bit values?

Multi-register values in this map use ABCD (high word first (big-endian)). Set your client to this order for every 32/64-bit value in the table.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Rishabh Instruments RISH EM 3490 SS single-phase energy meter?

Start with IR 0 Volts 1 (Voltage); IR 6 Current 1 (Current); IR 12 W1 (Active power); IR 30 PF1 (Power factor); IR 70 Frequency (Frequency); IR 72 Wh (active energy) (Active energy).

How do I enable Modbus control of the Rishabh Instruments RISH EM 3490 SS single-phase energy meter?

Modbus is an ordering option. Set node address, baud/parity (RS485 set-up code) and energy unit from the front panel or via 40019/40021/40005.
