Updated 19 November 2026

Short answer

Wall-mounted sample-draw refrigerant monitor for chiller and equipment rooms that checks up to 16 points for leaks of the common HVAC refrigerants. Over Modbus RTU it exposes 16-bit holding registers: unit status, an acknowledge and reset command, per-channel gas type, ppm reading and alarm flags, and calibration dates.

This page lists 76 Modbus registers for the Trane TruSense RMWH refrigerant monitor (up to 16 points) — 2 writable — with the table, 0-based wire address, data type, word order, scale, units and access of each, re-typed from Trane’s own free documentation (linked below) and checked against it.

Integration notes: Trane TruSense RMWH refrigerant monitor (up to 16 points)

Integration in brief

Select MODBUS on the Outputs > Digital tab, set the device address and baud rate, then poll holding registers 40007-40073. Each channel uses four registers: gas number, ppm, status flags, reserved.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

Digital output is None by default. During initial setup (or later on the Outputs screen) open the Digital tab, select MODBUS, press Edit, enter the Device Address, choose a baud rate, set the RS485 Termination check box as needed, and press Done.

Byte order & addressing

  • Channel n starts at register 40010 + 4 x (n - 1).
  • The reset command packs the action in the high byte and the channel (0-15, or 255 for all) in the low byte.

Network setup

  • RS-485 Modbus RTU only; set address and baud on the touchscreen.
  • The Modbus setup screen has an RS485 Termination check box for the built-in terminator.

Watch out for

  • Modbus is off until MODBUS is chosen on the Digital tab (factory default is None).
  • Calibration dates are day counts since 1970, not Unix seconds.

Notes on this map

  • The register list gives no data type or multiplier; every register is one 16-bit word, typed here as unsigned. Concentrations are listed in ppm as printed (the specifications give a 0-1000 ppm range and 1 ppm resolution).
  • RESET button actions (40009): high byte 0 + channel number (0-15) resets that channel's alarms, 0/255 resets all channel alarms; 1 + channel resets that channel's faults, 1/255 all channel faults; 2/255 resets common faults; 3/255 resets all alarms and faults.
  • Each channel also has a 'Reserved' register that reads 0 (40013, 40017 ... 40073); these and Reserved 40004-40006 are left out.
  • The document is marked 'Preliminary' and supersedes RMWH-SVX001D-EN.
  • Baud rate and device address are chosen on the touchscreen; the manual does not list the defaults, the baud choices or parity.
  • The table's 'Index' column starts at 40001 in 5-digit Modicon form; the manual gives no separate offset statement, so 40001 is taken as wire address 0.

Check it against the live device. A register map tells you what the controller can expose; the unit in front of you may differ by firmware or options. Read a few registers and compare them with the unit’s own display; for a 32-bit value the float and byte-order decoder shows all four word orders at once, so a wrong word order or scale is obvious. Questions about the Trane TruSense RMWH refrigerant monitor (up to 16 points)

What Modbus registers does the Trane TruSense RMWH refrigerant monitor (up to 16 points) have?

This map lists 76 registers: 76 holding registers. 2 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Trane TruSense RMWH refrigerant monitor (up to 16 points) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers Modicon 3xxxx/4xxxx (drop the prefix, subtract 1). Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. The register table's 'Index' column prints Modicon holding registers from 40001, so 40001 is wire address 0 and channel 1 gas concentration 40011 is wire address 10.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Trane TruSense RMWH refrigerant monitor (up to 16 points)?

Start with HR 6 Unit status (Unit status flags); HR 10 Channel 1 gas concentration (Channel 1 refrigerant ppm); HR 11 Channel 1 status (Channel 1 alarm flags); HR 9 Channel 1 gas number (Channel 1 gas type); HR 14 Channel 2 gas concentration (Channel 2 refrigerant ppm); HR 7 ACK button (Acknowledge alarms).

How do I enable Modbus control of the Trane TruSense RMWH refrigerant monitor (up to 16 points)?

Digital output is None by default. During initial setup (or later on the Outputs screen) open the Digital tab, select MODBUS, press Edit, enter the Device Address, choose a baud rate, set the RS485 Termination check box as needed, and press Done.
