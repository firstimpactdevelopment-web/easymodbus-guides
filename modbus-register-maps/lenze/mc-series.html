Lenze MC Series drives MC1000 / MC3000 (Modbus RTU) Modbus register map

Updated 17 November 2026

Short answer

The MC1000/MC3000 are Lenze AC Tech drives with a Modbus RTU slave at a fixed 9600 baud. Registers #1-#50 hold the control word, keypad speed command, status bytes (load, run status, direction, source, fault), motor voltage, run time and PID values; programming parameter n sits at register n + 51.

This page lists 20 Modbus registers for the Lenze MC Series drives MC1000 / MC3000 (Modbus RTU) — 8 writable — with the table, 0-based wire address, data type, word order, scale, units and access of each, re-typed from Lenze’s own free documentation (linked below) and checked against it.

Integration notes: Lenze MC Series drives MC1000 / MC3000 (Modbus RTU)

Integration in brief

Set P57 and P58 on the keypad. Read #26-#29 (or the 6-register block at #24) for load, status, mode, source and fault. To control: write the password (default 19) to #48, send 0200h to #1 for manual mode, start/stop via #1 and write the speed to #40.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

P57 SERIAL LINK = 01 (with 10 s watchdog) or 02 (without); P58 = address; P30 CONTROL usually LOCAL on MC1000 and SERIAL on MC3000 for serial use.

Communication loss & watchdog

Fixed 10 s once #48 or #49 has unlocked the drive and P57 = with timer; in LOCAL/KEYPAD 2 control the drive stops on time-out (no fault is logged).

Byte order & addressing

  • Read one register at a time except the #24 six-word block.
  • FC16 accepts a single register only; FC03 and FC04 are identical.
  • No broadcast support.

Network setup

  • Fixed 9600 baud, 8 data bits, no parity, 2 stop bits.

Watch out for

  • Changing CONTROL from LOCAL to REMOTE stops the drive immediately.
  • End a session by writing 2 to #1 (lock security).

Notes on this map

  • Host: copy hosted by Precision Electric (a Lenze AC Tech distributor); no copy was found on lenze.com.
  • Left out: command speed #24 and actual speed #25. Table 6 prints 0.1 Hz (0-2400), while the data-type table and notes 3a/3b say hundredths of a Hz (0x0201 = 5.13 Hz); the guide contradicts itself. #24 can still be read as a 6-register block starting at address 24.
  • Operational status (low byte #26): 0 fault lockout, 1 fault, 2 start pending, 3 stop, 4 DC brake, 5 run at 0 Hz, 6 run, 7 accel, 8 decel, 9 current limit, 10 decel override, 11 lower transistors switching on, 12 sleep.
  • Speed source (high byte #28): 0 keypad, 1 0-10 VDC, 2 4-20 mA, 3-6 preset 1-4, 7 MOP, 8 reserved, 9 keypad speed (PID enabled), 10 PID keypad setpoint, 11 0-10 VDC PID setpoint, 12 4-20 mA PID setpoint.
  • No kWh registers are listed for the MC Series. Only the control registers and serial-setup parameters are included here.
  • Present fault (high byte #29): 0 none, 1 output transistor, 3 high DC bus, 4 high drive temperature, 5 thermal overload, 7 low DC bus, 9 DC brake error, 10 follower loss, 11 dynamic brake overload, 12 power sag, 13 control, 14 language, 15 external, 16 internal 16, 17 power transient, 18-23 internal errors (2, 6 and 8 reserved).
  • P30 CONTROL: MC1000 0 local (default), 1 remote, 2 both; MC3000 0 local (default), 1 remote, 2 serial, 3 keypad, 4 TB strip, 5 keypad 2.

Questions about the Lenze MC Series drives MC1000 / MC3000 (Modbus RTU)

What Modbus registers does the Lenze MC Series drives MC1000 / MC3000 (Modbus RTU) have?

This map lists 20 registers: 20 holding registers. 8 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Lenze MC Series drives MC1000 / MC3000 (Modbus RTU) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers derived from parameter numbers. Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. Lenze numbers by AC Tech register #, which is the address sent on the wire (Modbus 4X/3X numbers are one larger). The frame examples confirm it: #24 is read at start address 0x0018. Programming parameter n = register n + 51.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Lenze MC Series drives MC1000 / MC3000 (Modbus RTU)?

Start with HR 48 Unlock commands (Unlock control); HR 1 Drive control (Start / stop / direction); HR 40 Keypad speed command (Keypad speed command); HR 27 Actual direction (high) / control mode (low) (Control mode (low byte) and direction); HR 26 Load (high byte) / operational status (low byte) (Load and status); HR 29 Present fault (high) / commanded direction (low) (Present fault).

How do I enable Modbus control of the Lenze MC Series drives MC1000 / MC3000 (Modbus RTU)?

P57 SERIAL LINK = 01 (with 10 s watchdog) or 02 (without); P58 = address; P30 CONTROL usually LOCAL on MC1000 and SERIAL on MC3000 for serial use.

What happens when the Lenze MC Series drives MC1000 / MC3000 (Modbus RTU) loses Modbus communication?

Fixed 10 s once #48 or #49 has unlocked the drive and P57 = with timer; in LOCAL/KEYPAD 2 control the drive stops on time-out (no fault is logged).
