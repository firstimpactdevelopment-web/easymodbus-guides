The Conto D1 is a one-module single-phase energy meter with an RS-485 Modbus/JBUS port. Its map is short: voltage, current, frequency and powers as scaled 16-bit words, two 32-bit energy totals, and the baud rate and node address.

Integration notes: IME Conto D1 single-phase energy meter (CE111)

Integration in brief

Read 6 holding registers from 0x00 (function 03) for V, A, Hz and powers, then 2 from 0x07 for active energy and 2 from 0x11 for reactive energy.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

RegisterNameTypeWhyAccess
HR 0VoltageUINT16VoltageRead
HR 1CurrentUINT16CurrentRead
HR 3Active powerUINT16Active powerRead
HR 7Total active energyUINT32 ABCDActive energyRead
HR 17Total reactive energyUINT32 ABCDReactive energyRead

Enable Modbus control & writes

Set node address and baud rate on the meter or through 0x2A/0x2B.

Byte order & addressing

  • A request may ask for at most 14 bytes of data (7 registers).
  • Only functions 03 and 0x10 are supported.

Network setup

  • Baud rate choices are 1200, 2400, 4800 and 9600; slave response time is at least 20 ms.

Watch out for

  • Energy registers count in tens of Wh/varh.

Notes on this map

  • Double words are sent most significant word first, as stated in the data format section with a worked example.

Questions about the IME Conto D1 single-phase energy meter (CE111)

What Modbus registers does the IME Conto D1 single-phase energy meter (CE111) have?

This map lists 10 registers: 10 holding registers. 2 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the IME Conto D1 single-phase energy meter (CE111) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers hexadecimal, 0-based. Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. Addresses are the hex word addresses sent as 'first WORD address' in the request, so 0x07 is wire address 7.

What byte order does the IME Conto D1 single-phase energy meter (CE111) use for 32-bit values?

Multi-register values in this map use ABCD (high word first (big-endian)). Set your client to this order for every 32/64-bit value in the table.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the IME Conto D1 single-phase energy meter (CE111)?

Start with HR 0 Voltage (Voltage); HR 1 Current (Current); HR 3 Active power (Active power); HR 7 Total active energy (Active energy); HR 17 Total reactive energy (Reactive energy).

How do I enable Modbus control of the IME Conto D1 single-phase energy meter (CE111)?

Set node address and baud rate on the meter or through 0x2A/0x2B.

Where is the official IME Conto D1 single-phase energy meter (CE111) Modbus documentation?
