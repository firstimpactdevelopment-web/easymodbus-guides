Short answer

The WattNode Modbus is a compact three-phase energy meter with external CTs and an RS-485 Modbus RTU port. Most values come both as 32-bit floats and as scaled integers; 32-bit values are sent low word first. This map covers energy (net, positive, negative, reactive, apparent), power, voltage, current, PF, frequency, demand, CT and demand configuration, communication overrides and diagnostics.

This page lists 107 Modbus registers for the Continental Control Systems WattNode Modbus (WNC) energy meter — 44 writable — with the table, 0-based wire address, data type, word order, scale, units and access of each, re-typed from Continental Control Systems’s own free documentation (linked below) and checked against it.

Integration notes: Continental Control Systems WattNode Modbus (WNC) energy meter

Integration in brief

Set the address and baud on the DIP switches, set CtAmps (1603), then read floats from 1001-1034 (documented numbers; wire 1000-1033) in one request, decoding each pair low word first.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

Modbus RTU on RS-485 is always on. Address and baud come from the DIP switches unless overridden at 1652-1656 (write 1234 to 1651 to apply).

Byte order & addressing

  • 32-bit values are low word first (CDAB).
  • Documented numbers are one-based; subtract 1 on the wire.
  • Float energy loses resolution above 100,000 kWh; the integer energy registers keep 0.1 kWh.

Network setup

  • Default 9600 baud (DIP switch 8 down), N81.
  • Set CtAmps (1603) to the CT rating before reading power.

Watch out for

  • A configuration passcode (1601) blocks writes if one was set.
  • Writing a 32-bit register with two single writes must happen within 30 s with nothing else in between.

Notes on this map

  • Word order: the manual says the first (lower) register of a float or 32-bit integer holds the least significant 16 bits (little-endian, 'byte order 1032' on Tridium), so 32-bit values are CDAB.
  • Holding and input registers are interchangeable (functions 03 and 04 return the same data).
  • Left out: ConfigPasscode (1601-1602, signedness not stated); integer power, current and demand registers (1209-1212, 1343-1360), which use the configurable PowerIntScale/CurrentIntScale factors; integer per-phase energies; gain/phase adjustment, creep and I/O mode settings; option information and custom register map registers.
  • Read both halves of a 32-bit value in one request; the meter only guarantees a consistent pair within a single read.
  • The Model register lists codes only for WNC models; revenue RWNC codes are not given.
  • SerialNumber, UptimeSecs and TotalSecs are described as 32-bit long integers, which the manual defines as signed, so they are INT32.

Check it against the live device. A register map tells you what the controller can expose; the unit in front of you may differ by firmware or options. Read a few registers and compare them with the unit’s own display; for a 32-bit value the float and byte-order decoder shows all four word orders at once, so a wrong word order or scale is obvious. Questions about the Continental Control Systems WattNode Modbus (WNC) energy meter

What Modbus registers does the Continental Control Systems WattNode Modbus (WNC) energy meter have?

This map lists 107 registers: 107 holding registers. 44 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Continental Control Systems WattNode Modbus (WNC) energy meter Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers 1-based (subtract 1 for the wire address). Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. The manual documents registers one-based and says they are sent zero-based: EnergySum is documented at 1001 and appears on the bus as 1000 (0x03E8). Wire address = documented number - 1.

What byte order does the Continental Control Systems WattNode Modbus (WNC) energy meter use for 32-bit values?

Multi-register values in this map use CDAB (low word first (word-swapped)). Set your client to this order for every 32/64-bit value in the table.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Continental Control Systems WattNode Modbus (WNC) energy meter?

Start with HR 1000 EnergySum (EnergySum (kWh)); HR 1008 PowerSum (PowerSum (W)); HR 1016 VoltAvgLN (Average L-N voltage); HR 1162 CurrentA (Current phase A); HR 1138 PowerFactorAvg (Average power factor); HR 1032 Freq (Frequency); HR 1602 CtAmps (CtAmps (must match the CTs)).

How do I enable Modbus control of the Continental Control Systems WattNode Modbus (WNC) energy meter?

Modbus RTU on RS-485 is always on. Address and baud come from the DIP switches unless overridden at 1652-1656 (write 1234 to 1651 to apply).

Where is the official Continental Control Systems WattNode Modbus (WNC) energy meter Modbus documentation?

The source is WattNode Modbus Installation and Operation Manual (WNC-MB-1.18c), revision 2015-11-03, published free by Continental Control Systems at https://ctlsys.com/m/WNC-MB-Ref-Manual.pdf.
