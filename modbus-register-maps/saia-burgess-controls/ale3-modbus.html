Short answer

The ALE3 is a Saia Burgess Controls DIN-rail three-phase active energy meter for direct connection up to 65 A, with an RS-485 Modbus RTU interface. Baud rate and parity are detected automatically. This map covers identification, status and error registers, the two total and two resettable partial energy counters, and per-phase voltage and current.

Integration notes: Saia Burgess Controls ALE3 three-phase 65 A energy meter (Modbus RTU)

Integration in brief

Read holding registers 28-33 (wire 27-32) for the energy counters as 32-bit high-word-first values in 0.01 kWh, and 36-47 for voltages and currents; keep each request at 20 registers or fewer.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

Set the Modbus address on the meter (press the touch key for 3 s, then step the address) or write register 24; baud rate and parity are detected automatically.

Byte order & addressing

  • Register R is wire address R - 1.
  • Max 20 registers per read; only FC03 and FC16.
  • Address register cannot be written by broadcast.

Network setup

  • The interface has no built-in terminating resistor; fit one externally.
  • Up to 247 meters per bus; use a repeater beyond 128.

Watch out for

  • Reset partial counter WT1 by writing 0 to both of its registers (30-31) in one FC16 message.
  • Data refreshes every 10 s; the sheet advises not polling one meter faster than that.
  • The interface works only with phase 1 connected and answers 30 s after power-up.

Notes on this map

  • This is an unaltered copy of the SBC instruction sheet posted on the Viessmann community forum; an official public SBC URL for this revision was not found in the time available.
  • The sheet lists the double registers sent high register first (big-endian, ABCD) as 4-5, 16-17, 28-29, 30-31 and 32-33. Example from the sheet: high 13, low 60383 = 912351 = 9123.51 kWh.
  • Energy counters are 0.01 kWh per count. Voltage is 1 V per count, current 0.1 A per count.
  • On the unidirectional type registers 28-35 are tariff 1 and tariff 2 counters. On the bidirectional type the sheet's display section describes the second counter set as feed-back (delivered) energy; the tariff register's arrows mark direction.
  • Left out: active and reactive power and cos phi per phase and total (registers 38-40, 43-45, 48-52), because the sheet does not say whether these 16-bit values are signed, which matters on the bidirectional type; the serial number (16-18), whose word layout is printed inconsistently; type/ASN text registers 7-14.
  • Left out: partial energy WT2 (registers 34-35). The sheet names it a resettable partial counter but leaves it off its list of high-register-first pairs, so its word order is not stated.
  • Only functions 03 and 16 are accepted, and at most 20 registers per request.
  • Serial format: baud rate (4800-115200) and parity are auto-detected; even or odd parity use 1 stop bit, no parity uses 2 stop bits.

Check it against the live device. A register map tells you what the controller can expose; the unit in front of you may differ by firmware or options. Read a few registers and compare them with the unit’s own display; for a 32-bit value the float and byte-order decoder shows all four word orders at once, so a wrong word order or scale is obvious. Questions about the Saia Burgess Controls ALE3 three-phase 65 A energy meter (Modbus RTU)

What Modbus registers does the Saia Burgess Controls ALE3 three-phase 65 A energy meter (Modbus RTU) have?

This map lists 18 registers: 18 holding registers. 2 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Saia Burgess Controls ALE3 three-phase 65 A energy meter (Modbus RTU) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers 1-based (subtract 1 for the wire address). Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. The register list numbers registers from 1, and the manual states that register R is sent as R - 1: register 28 (energy total) is wire address 27.

What byte order does the Saia Burgess Controls ALE3 three-phase 65 A energy meter (Modbus RTU) use for 32-bit values?

Multi-register values in this map use ABCD (high word first (big-endian)). Set your client to this order for every 32/64-bit value in the table.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Saia Burgess Controls ALE3 three-phase 65 A energy meter (Modbus RTU)?

Start with HR 27 Counter total energy WT1 (Total energy WT1, 0.01 kWh); HR 31 Counter total energy WT2 (Total energy WT2, 0.01 kWh); HR 35 Voltage phase 1 (URMS) (Voltage L1); HR 36 Current phase 1 (IRMS) (Current L1, 0.1 A); HR 24 Error register (Error register).

How do I enable Modbus control of the Saia Burgess Controls ALE3 three-phase 65 A energy meter (Modbus RTU)?

Set the Modbus address on the meter (press the touch key for 3 s, then step the address) or write register 24; baud rate and parity are detected automatically.
