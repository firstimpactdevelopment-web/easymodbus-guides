Short answer

The indoor-unit controller of Aermec LPG units answers Modbus RTU directly, so no extra gateway is needed. Holding registers carry on/off, mode, fan speed, setpoint, louvre position and temperatures; coils carry feature flags (turbo, silent, locks, energy saving) and a long list of indoor, outdoor and inverter-drive fault bits. Up to 255 units can share a BMS line.

This page lists 114 Modbus registers for the Aermec LPG series DC inverter air conditioners (built-in Modbus) — 30 writable — with the table, 0-based wire address, data type, word order, scale, units and access of each, re-typed from Aermec’s own free documentation (linked below) and checked against it.

Integration notes: Aermec LPG series DC inverter air conditioners (built-in Modbus)

Integration in brief

Read words 2-25 for on/off, mode, fan, setpoint and louvres and word 4/49 for indoor and outdoor temperature; read coils 0-215 for status and fault bits. Write word 2 (0xAA/0x55), 17, 19 and 20 to control the unit.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

The Modbus interface is built into the indoor-unit controller; the unit address and BMS mode are set from the wired controller (see the unit's service manual).

Byte order & addressing

  • Writes are refused while an energy-saving limit blocks the requested setpoint; the BMS should check the result.
  • Energy saving bits 32 and 33 must be written together.
  • Fault code 0x04 in an exception reply means the interface lost contact with the unit; it is also returned after power-up until valid data arrives.

Network setup

  • RS485, 9600 baud, 8 data bits, no parity, 1 stop bit.
  • Twisted pair, kept at least 15 cm from power cables; use repeaters for long lines.

Watch out for

  • In fan or auto mode the sleep setting is ignored; in auto mode the remote temperature shield is ignored.
  • Heating commands to cooling-only units (bit 177 = 1) have no effect.

Questions about the Aermec LPG series DC inverter air conditioners (built-in Modbus)

What Modbus registers does the Aermec LPG series DC inverter air conditioners (built-in Modbus) have?

This map lists 114 registers: 96 coils, 18 holding registers. 30 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Aermec LPG series DC inverter air conditioners (built-in Modbus) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers 0-based (the number printed is the address on the wire). Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. The protocol says registers are words numbered from 0 and coils are bits numbered from 0 (Byte0.0 = bit 0). Word n is wire address n and Bit n is coil n; the example frames agree (read starting at word 1 sends 0x0001, coil 5 sends 0x0005).

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Aermec LPG series DC inverter air conditioners (built-in Modbus)?

Start with HR 2 Unit On/Off (On/off); HR 17 Set mode (Mode); HR 19 Set fan speed (Fan speed); HR 20 Set temp (Setpoint); HR 4 Ambient temp (Indoor temperature); HR 49 Outdoor ambient temp (Outdoor temperature); CO 151 Compressor status (Compressor running); CO 95 Error output status (Error output).

How do I enable Modbus control of the Aermec LPG series DC inverter air conditioners (built-in Modbus)?

The Modbus interface is built into the indoor-unit controller; the unit address and BMS mode are set from the wired controller (see the unit's service manual).
