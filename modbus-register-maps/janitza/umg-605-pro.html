Short answer

The UMG 605-PRO is a Janitza power quality analyser with an RS-485 port and network (IP) settings. Measurements are big-endian 32-bit floats; the 19000-19120 'frequently required readings' block holds volts, amps, power, CosPhi, frequency, energy and THD, the same block as other Janitza meters. Its other address blocks differ from the UMG 604-PRO, so this map lists the 605-PRO's own addresses for the fourth channel, consumed energy, digital I/O, energy resets, transformer settings and network values.

This page lists 73 Modbus registers for the Janitza UMG 605-PRO power quality analyser (Modbus RTU / Modbus TCP) — 2 writable — with the table, 0-based wire address, data type, word order, scale, units and access of each, re-typed from Janitza’s own free documentation (linked below) and checked against it.

Integration notes: Janitza UMG 605-PRO power quality analyser (Modbus RTU / Modbus TCP)

Integration in brief

Read holding registers (function 03) from 19000: 60 registers give volts, amps, power, CosPhi, frequency and per-phase energy as big-endian floats, and 19060-19077 adds three-phase and delivered energy in Wh. Read 6508 for the consumed energy total.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

Modbus is available on Ethernet and on the RS-485 port; set the Modbus address and IP settings on the device or in GridVis (see the device manual).

Byte order & addressing

  • Addresses are used as printed (0-based).
  • Floats are ABCD; add 32768 to an address for little-endian.
  • Energy is in Wh and varh, not kWh.
  • Rotation field (19052) is a float: 1, 0 or -1.

Network setup

  • RS-485 on the device itself: 8 data bits, no parity, 2 stop bits; baud 9.6, 19.2, 38.4, 57.6, 115.2 or 921.6 kbps.
  • CT and VT ratios are one setting for L1-L3 plus one for L4.

Watch out for

  • 19068 in the frequent block is tariff 1 consumed energy only; use 6508 for the full consumed total.
  • The 605-PRO and 604-PRO share the 19000 block but nothing else; check which list you are using.

Notes on this map

  • Byte order: the list says its addresses return data big-endian (high byte first, high word in the lower register = ABCD). Adding 32768 to an address returns the same value little-endian; that mirror block is not listed.
  • Do not reuse UMG 604-PRO addresses outside the 19000 block: on the 605-PRO the main values, energy and settings sit at different addresses (e.g. consumed energy total 6508 instead of 9851).
  • Access: only the 19000-19716 area carries an RD/WR column (the measured values there are RD). Elsewhere the list has no access column; only the delete commands ('1 = delete') are given write access here.
  • Left out: 19068 (consumed L1-L3 on tariff 1 only); the consumed total is at 6508 (_WH_V[4]).
  • Left out: 19636 (_PF_TOTAL) because its printed remark runs into the 'internal temperature' text of the row above, so the row is unclear.
  • Left out: serial number, Modbus address, RS-485 mode and baud rate (6856-6868), which the list marks 'only for internal use', plus harmonics, min/max/mean, event and display settings.
  • Measured values refresh every 200 ms.

Questions about the Janitza UMG 605-PRO power quality analyser (Modbus RTU / Modbus TCP)

What Modbus registers does the Janitza UMG 605-PRO power quality analyser (Modbus RTU / Modbus TCP) have?

This map lists 73 registers: 73 holding registers. 2 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Janitza UMG 605-PRO power quality analyser (Modbus RTU / Modbus TCP) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers 0-based (the number printed is the address on the wire). Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. Addresses are printed as the protocol address and sent unchanged: 19000 is requested as 19000. The list starts at address 0 (system time), which only fits 0-based numbering. In a 1-based or Modicon tool enter 19001 / 419001.

What byte order does the Janitza UMG 605-PRO power quality analyser (Modbus RTU / Modbus TCP) use for 32-bit values?

Multi-register values in this map use ABCD (high word first (big-endian)). Set your client to this order for every 32/64-bit value in the table.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Janitza UMG 605-PRO power quality analyser (Modbus RTU / Modbus TCP)?

Start with HR 19000 Voltage L1-N (Voltage L1-N); HR 19012 Current L1 (Current L1); HR 19026 Real power total L1-L3 (Total real power); HR 19034 Apparent power total L1-L3 (Total apparent power); HR 3929 CosPhi total L1-L3 (fundamental) (Total CosPhi); HR 19050 Frequency (Frequency); HR 6508 Real energy L1-L3, consumed (Consumed energy total); HR 19076 Real energy L1-L3, delivered (Delivered energy total).
