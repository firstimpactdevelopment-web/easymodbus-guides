Senva EM-RS485 networked energy meter (Modbus RTU/ASCII) Modbus register map

Short answer

The Senva EM-RS485 is a compact three-phase energy meter that reads Senva current/voltage transducers (CVTs) and talks Modbus RTU, Modbus ASCII or BACnet MS/TP on RS-485 with auto-detected line settings. Measurements are IEEE floats in holding registers: system and per-phase voltage, current, power factor, real/reactive/apparent power, energy, demand, plus condition flags. This map covers the main reading and setup registers.

Integration notes: Senva EM-RS485 networked energy meter (Modbus RTU/ASCII)

Integration in brief

Connect RS-485 to an active Modbus master and let the meter auto-detect the line, then read the float pairs at R600 (total kW), R700 (kWh), R420 (V) and R520 (A) with function 03.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

Modbus is on by default (protocol Auto). Lock baud/parity/protocol with R122-R128 for permanent installations.

Byte order & addressing

  • Read and write both registers of a 32-bit value together.
  • Units change with R163/R173/R133/R153; check them before scaling.
  • The meter answers the global address 255 as well as its own address.

Network setup

  • Default address = 100 + last two digits of the serial number.
  • Hold the Setup button while powering up to clear learned line settings.

Watch out for

  • Writing a new baud rate cuts communication until the master matches it.
  • On a mixed-baud network auto-detection is unpredictable; set the line settings manually.

Notes on this map

  • Word order: the data-types section says UINT32 and FLOAT use two registers in network byte order and gives byte examples (0xAABBCCDD sent as AA BB CC DD across registers r and r+1), so the high word comes first (ABCD).
  • Units are settable: power follows R163 (default kW), energy R173 (default kWh), temperature R133 (default °F), angles R153 (default degrees). The units listed here are the defaults.
  • Energy registers (R700-R765, R1700-R1765) read as floats by default; with R170 = 1 they read as unsigned integers 0-999999 that wrap. They are listed as FLOAT32 here (the default).
  • Most analog measurements also have Instantaneous, Minimum, Maximum and Average copies in the next registers (for example R422 instantaneous, R424 min, R426 max, R428 average for R420); those copies are not listed. Writing 0 resets a statistic.
  • Default slave address is 100 plus the last two digits of the serial number. Baud (9600-115200), parity, data bits and protocol are auto-detected out of the box.
  • Strings (CVT serial/model, location) and the remaining PowerPrint, pulse-input and multiplier settings are left out for length.

Check it against the live device. A register map tells you what the controller can expose; the unit in front of you may differ by firmware or options. Read a few registers and compare them with the unit’s own display; for a 32-bit value the float and byte-order decoder shows all four word orders at once, so a wrong word order or scale is obvious. Questions about the Senva EM-RS485 networked energy meter (Modbus RTU/ASCII)

What Modbus registers does the Senva EM-RS485 networked energy meter (Modbus RTU/ASCII) have?

This map lists 83 registers: 83 holding registers. 18 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the Senva EM-RS485 networked energy meter (Modbus RTU/ASCII) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers 0-based (the number printed is the address on the wire). Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. The guide prints raw protocol addresses with an R prefix (R420 = wire address 420; Modicon 40421). R420/21 marks a two-register value starting at 420. S phase registers are R phase + 1000, T phase + 2000.

What byte order does the Senva EM-RS485 networked energy meter (Modbus RTU/ASCII) use for 32-bit values?

Multi-register values in this map use ABCD (high word first (big-endian)). Set your client to this order for every 32/64-bit value in the table.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the Senva EM-RS485 networked energy meter (Modbus RTU/ASCII)?

Start with HR 600 Total real power (Total real power); HR 700 Total real energy (Total real energy); HR 420 Phase average RMS voltage (Average voltage); HR 520 Phase average RMS current (Average current); HR 550 System power factor (Power factor); HR 410 Line frequency (Frequency); HR 400 Active system conditions (System condition flags).

How do I enable Modbus control of the Senva EM-RS485 networked energy meter (Modbus RTU/ASCII)?

Modbus is on by default (protocol Auto). Lock baud/parity/protocol with R122-R128 for permanent installations.
