Short answer

The SEN0662 is a maker-grade particulate sensor board built on the Bosch BMV080. With its DIP switch on the UART side it acts as a Modbus RTU slave. Input registers give PM1.0, PM2.5 and PM10 as big-endian floats plus status; holding registers set the address, serial format and measurement mode and start or stop measurement.

This page lists 26 Modbus registers for the DFRobot SEN0662 Gravity BMV080 PM2.5 air quality sensor (UART Modbus RTU) — 10 writable — with the table, 0-based wire address, data type, word order, scale, units and access of each, re-typed from DFRobot’s own free documentation (linked below) and checked against it.

Integration notes: DFRobot SEN0662 Gravity BMV080 PM2.5 air quality sensor (UART Modbus RTU)

Integration in brief

At 9600 8N1, address 0x57: write 0x0001 to holding register 0x0003 to start measuring, then read six input registers from 0x0006 with function 04 for PM1.0, PM2.5 and PM10 as big-endian floats in ug/m3.

Minimum registers to map

Addresses are 0-based wire addresses. Enough to monitor and run the unit; map more as the job needs.

Enable Modbus control & writes

Set the module's DIP switch to the UART side; Modbus RTU is then active at the stored address and baud rate.

Byte order & addressing

  • Measurement must be started after every power cycle.
  • Baud and parity changes take effect after a reboot.
  • Changing the algorithm restarts measurement; blockage and vibration settings are re-applied when changed. After changing the measurement mode, send a start command.

Network setup

  • Defaults: 9600 baud, 8 data bits, no parity, 1 stop bit, address 0x57.
  • The wiki wiring example connects the board R/T pins to a UART adapter (RainbowLink V2).

Watch out for

  • If a PM value reads 0 or NaN, check bit 9 of status flags (0x000E).
  • Full-scale PM range is 1000 ug/m3; bit 1 of status flags flags PM2.5 out of range.

Notes on this map

  • Word order: the wiki says registers are big-endian and the high word comes first, and its example response 40 E0 00 00 decodes to 7.0 ug/m3, so floats are ABCD.
  • The default slave address is 0x57 (decimal 87). The board's I2C address pins do not affect it.
  • The sensor does not measure after power-up: write 1 to holding register 0x0003 after every power cycle before reading PM values.
  • The Integration Time float has no unit printed in the table; it is listed without a unit.
  • The wiki also documents a broadcast read of holding register 0x0000 (address scan) that only works with a single device on the bus; it is a vendor extension, not standard Modbus.

Check it against the live device. A register map tells you what the controller can expose; the unit in front of you may differ by firmware or options. Read a few registers and compare them with the unit’s own display; for a 32-bit value the float and byte-order decoder shows all four word orders at once, so a wrong word order or scale is obvious. Questions about the DFRobot SEN0662 Gravity BMV080 PM2.5 air quality sensor (UART Modbus RTU)

What Modbus registers does the DFRobot SEN0662 Gravity BMV080 PM2.5 air quality sensor (UART Modbus RTU) have?

This map lists 26 registers: 16 input registers, 10 holding registers. 10 of them can be written. Registers vary with firmware, options and configuration, so confirm against the device.

Are the DFRobot SEN0662 Gravity BMV080 PM2.5 air quality sensor (UART Modbus RTU) Modbus addresses 0-based or 1-based?

The manufacturer's document numbers registers hexadecimal, 0-based. Every address on this page is the 0-based wire address you put in the request; the documented number is shown under it where it differs. Registers are listed as hex protocol addresses from 0x0000 and sent unchanged; the wiki's example reads PM1.0 with start address 0x0006 on function 04.

What byte order does the DFRobot SEN0662 Gravity BMV080 PM2.5 air quality sensor (UART Modbus RTU) use for 32-bit values?

Multi-register values in this map use ABCD (high word first (big-endian)). Set your client to this order for every 32/64-bit value in the table.

What are the minimum Modbus registers to integrate the DFRobot SEN0662 Gravity BMV080 PM2.5 air quality sensor (UART Modbus RTU)?

Start with HR 3 Operation Command (Start measurement command); IR 6 PM1.0 (PM1.0); IR 8 PM2.5 (PM2.5); IR 10 PM10 (PM10); IR 14 Status Flags (Status flags); IR 4 Operating Status (Operating status).

How do I enable Modbus control of the DFRobot SEN0662 Gravity BMV080 PM2.5 air quality sensor (UART Modbus RTU)?

Set the module's DIP switch to the UART side; Modbus RTU is then active at the stored address and baud rate.
